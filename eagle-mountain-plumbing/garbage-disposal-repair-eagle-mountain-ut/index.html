    Leak Detection and Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Eagle Mountain

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.
