    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Eagle Mountain

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. Then we fix the source. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Eagle Mountain

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head.
