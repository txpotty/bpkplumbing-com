    We typically install Type K copper, PEX rated for direct burial or HDPE for trenchless pulls. Each holds up well in most soils when properly bedded. We will recommend the right material based on your soil, line length and budget.

    Full service line replacement typically requires a plumbing permit and inspection from your city or county. Small spot repairs often do not. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when one is required.

    For water line replacements, we mainly use durable and code-compliant materials such as PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper.
