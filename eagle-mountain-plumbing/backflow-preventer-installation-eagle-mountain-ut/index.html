    Reliable Backflow Preventer Installation Services in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Eagle Mountain

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Eagle Mountain

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Eagle Mountain, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Eagle Mountain

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water utility.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Eagle Mountain, UT

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a direct path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Eagle Mountain, UT

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost significantly more in parts and labor. Size, brand, enclosure needs and how much existing pipe must be modified all affect the price. Replacing a failed old device with a new one is usually cheaper than repeated rebuilds. We give you an itemized quote that includes the initial certification test.

BPK Plumbing offers emergency replacement service to quickly assess the situation, remove the faulty unit and install a new, code-compliant backflow preventer, restoring your water safety as fast as possible.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Eagle Mountain

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.
