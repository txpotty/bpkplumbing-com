    Finding and Fixing Slab Leaks in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Eagle Mountain

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. Look for a rising bill, a meter that spins with nothing on or damp carpet, since a cold leak wets the soil as much as a hot one. Your Eagle Mountain home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Eagle Mountain

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Residential Slab Leak Repair in Eagle Mountain

    Working in an occupied home means planning around your day. Drop cloths go down first. Dust gets contained with plastic sheeting, the cut area is kept small, and the water stays on for the rest of the house while we work on one line, so you can still cook and shower. Eagle Mountain homeowners find the area swept and patched when we leave.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Eagle Mountain

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Paired with acoustic listening gear, it lets us mark a leak in copper, PEX or galvanized pipe even near busy roads, because the radar fixes the pipe route and the mic confirms the leak. We use both in Eagle Mountain.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Eagle Mountain

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Eagle Mountain homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Slab Leak Detection in Eagle Mountain
