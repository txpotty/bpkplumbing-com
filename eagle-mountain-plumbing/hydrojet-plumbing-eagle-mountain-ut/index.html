    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Eagle Mountain, UT can range from $300 to $800 or more, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe.     Key signs you might need hydrojet plumbing include often recurring clogs that snaking doesn't fully resolve, multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, or foul odors coming from your drains. A severe sewer backup is also a strong indicator that hydrojetting is needed to clear the main line.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, often leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more complete and longer-lasting solution.

    We use professional-grade hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers like Spartan Tool, US Jetting, and Gorlitz. These brands are known for their reliability, power, and effectiveness in clearing even the toughest pipe blockages, delivering a thorough clean every time.

    Most homes only need jetting every few years or when clogs recur. Restaurants and busy commercial kitchens often benefit from jetting every three to six months. We recommend a schedule based on what the camera shows.
