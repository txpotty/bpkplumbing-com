    Softener Systems We Replace in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Eagle Mountain

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Utah homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Softener Loop Installation in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Eagle Mountain

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.
