    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Eagle Mountain, UT, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Eagle Mountain

    Spring snowmelt can raise groundwater and creek levels in Utah, sometimes pushing water into basements that stay dry the rest of the year. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Eagle Mountain provides an important layer of protection.     Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Eagle Mountain

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Eagle Mountain

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Eagle Mountain

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too often or failing under stress, common issues in some Eagle Mountain properties.
