    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Eagle Mountain, UT

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Installed tanks often run well over a thousand dollars, and regular flushing and anode replacement help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan, while tankless units can last many years with proper descaling. Sometimes replacement makes sense. We give you real numbers for both.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Eagle Mountain

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Eagle Mountain

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Electric Element Service in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. Good parts stay in.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. High demand speeds up scale and wear, so commercial units need service on a tighter schedule. We service them regularly. Hot water stays on for your business.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Eagle Mountain

    Businesses cannot run without hot water. Restaurants, salons and gyms in Eagle Mountain lean on large tanks or banks of tankless units, and a failure during business hours means closing a kitchen or sending customers home. We schedule around your hours. Larger units get the same flush, anode and safety checks.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Eagle Mountain

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Eagle Mountain, UT

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
