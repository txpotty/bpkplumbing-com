    Well Pump Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    No water at the tap on a well system narrows the list quickly. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. We check voltage and amp draw at the control box before pulling anything out of the well. Pulling a submersible pump is a two-person job.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Pinhole leaks in copper begin inside the pipe, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Where there is one pinhole, there are often more nearby. The fix is a new section soldered or pressed in, plus a pressure check at a hose bib. Above 80 psi, add a regulator.

    Well Pump Service in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Submersible pumps sit well below the water line, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. BPK Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and most of the time a bad foot valve or a lost prime is to blame.
