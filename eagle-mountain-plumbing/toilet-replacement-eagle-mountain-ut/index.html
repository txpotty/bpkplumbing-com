    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Eagle Mountain, UT

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models.     Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Eagle Mountain

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs.
