    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Eagle Mountain

    Most residential expansion tank installations cost $250 to $550 including the tank and labor. Attic installs, larger tanks or a new pressure reducing valve can bring the total to $600 to $1,000.

    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior or a water heater that frequently cycles on and off. You might also notice fluctuating water pressure or a banging sound in your pipes, which indicates excessive pressure buildup.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve, check valve or backflow preventer on the main, you probably have a closed system that needs one. Many local codes require it whenever a water heater is installed on a closed system.

    An expansion tank is necessary in closed plumbing systems to absorb the increased water volume and pressure that occur when water is heated. Without it, thermal expansion can damage your water heater, pipes and fixtures, leading to leaks and premature system failure. Many local codes in Utah call for them.

    BPK Plumbing installs high-quality expansion tanks from reputable brands like Watts, Amtrol and Zurn. We select brands known for durability and performance so your system gets the best protection. Our plumbers can recommend the best option for your water heater and plumbing setup.

    While less common than with traditional tank water heaters, an expansion tank can still be required or beneficial for tankless water heaters, especially if your home has a closed plumbing system because of a backflow preventer or pressure reducing valve. We will evaluate your setup to provide the best recommendation.

    We install potable-rated tanks from Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Each is approved for drinking water and made with corrosion-resistant connections.

    A standalone expansion tank usually does not require a permit, but one is often included when it is installed with a new water heater. We follow local requirements on every job.
