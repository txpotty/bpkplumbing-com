    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Eagle Mountain, UT

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Eagle Mountain, UT. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.
