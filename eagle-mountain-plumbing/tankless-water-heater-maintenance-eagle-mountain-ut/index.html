    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Eagle Mountain

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit usually runs $150 to $300 more.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can cancel the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your own warranty terms.

    Many manufacturers exclude scale damage from heat exchanger warranties. Regular, documented maintenance is the best way to keep your coverage intact.

    If you have isolation valves, a pump and the right solution, a handy homeowner can do it. A pro visit also checks gas pressure, venting, sensors and condensate, which a DIY flush leaves out.

    While some basic tasks like cleaning external filters might be possible for a homeowner, full maintenance, especially descaling the heat exchanger and inspecting gas lines, should be left to a licensed professional. Improper servicing can damage the unit, void warranties and create safety risks related to gas and carbon monoxide.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds about an hour.

    Most tankless water heater manufacturers recommend annual maintenance to ensure good performance and extend the unit's lifespan. In areas with harder water, more frequent checks, perhaps every 6 to 9 months, might help to prevent excessive scale buildup.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in less than half that time.
