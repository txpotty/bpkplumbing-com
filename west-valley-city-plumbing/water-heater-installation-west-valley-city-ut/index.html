    Gas Water Heater Replacement in West Valley City

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in West Valley City, UT

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in West Valley City, UT

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in West Valley City, UT

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. First step is to close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the gas or the breaker, which stops the leak from getting worse. We carry common sizes. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in West Valley City, UT

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in West Valley City

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Backflow prevention is taken seriously on irrigation systems here, and BPK Plumbing can check and repair assemblies to meet local requirements. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Expansion Tank Installation in West Valley City, UT

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
