    Jet Pump System Repair in West Valley City, UT

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. BPK Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.
