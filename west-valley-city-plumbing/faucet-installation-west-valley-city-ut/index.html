    Touchless Faucet Installation in West Valley City

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. We mount the control box high on the cabinet wall, away from where water collects, and power it with batteries or a plug-in adapter.     Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in West Valley City

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. We mount wall or deck models with an integral vacuum breaker, since a hose left in a sink full of soapy water is a textbook backflow risk. We clip the supply lines so nothing rubs.
