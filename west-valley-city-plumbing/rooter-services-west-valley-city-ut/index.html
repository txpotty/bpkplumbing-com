    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in West Valley City

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. Nights and weekends run higher.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. They also get stuck. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.

    Rooting is safe with a trained tech and the correct cable and blade. Collapsed or badly cracked pipe can be hurt by anything run through it. The tech's touch matters. If the cable tells us the pipe is damaged, we stop and send in a camera before going further.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. A jetter uses water at thousands of psi to scour the inside of the pipe from wall to wall. Jetting leaves the pipe cleaner. Snaking handles one-time clogs, while jetting is for grease lines and scaled mains that keep coming back.

    Clearing a clog in West Valley City does not need a permit. Repair work is different. If the camera shows broken pipe that needs a dig, a new cleanout or a sewer replacement, a plumbing permit is usually required. We handle the permit and the inspection.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds and wipes out of drains. Put strainers in tub and shower drains to catch hair. Run hot water after using the disposal, and scrape plates into the trash before rinsing so less food goes down the kitchen line. Root-prone lines need cutting every year or two.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access is the wild card. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.

    Every rooter job from BPK Plumbing carries a guarantee for a set period on the line we cleared. We go over the terms before we start. Coverage is specific. Should that line clog again during the guarantee window, the return visit is free.
