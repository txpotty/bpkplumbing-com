    Type L vs. Type K Copper in West Valley City, UT

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in West Valley City, UT

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in West Valley City, UT

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in West Valley City

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it.
