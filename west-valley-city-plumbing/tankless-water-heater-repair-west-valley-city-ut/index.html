    Electric Tankless Element Repair in West Valley City, UT

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in West Valley City, UT

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in West Valley City

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that drops out of hard water when it heats, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We hook a small pump and hoses to the isolation valves and circulate descaler for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in West Valley City

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in West Valley City

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Most tankless installs include isolation valves with purge ports for exactly this job, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. Vinegar works in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in West Valley City, UT

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. A breaker that trips under load can mean a shorted element or a loose lug that has been arcing, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. We check element resistance and supply voltage. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in West Valley City, UT

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.
