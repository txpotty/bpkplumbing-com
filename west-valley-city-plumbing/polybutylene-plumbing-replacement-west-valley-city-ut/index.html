    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in West Valley City

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened.
