    Why Polybutylene Fails in West Valley City

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    PEX Pipe Installation in West Valley City, UT

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in West Valley City

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in West Valley City, UT
