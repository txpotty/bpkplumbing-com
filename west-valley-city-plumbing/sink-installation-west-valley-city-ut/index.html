    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in West Valley City

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Undermount Sink Installation in West Valley City

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in West Valley City, UT
