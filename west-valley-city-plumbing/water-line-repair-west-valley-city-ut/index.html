    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in West Valley City

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    PEX Water Line Repair in West Valley City, UT

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in West Valley City, UT

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.
