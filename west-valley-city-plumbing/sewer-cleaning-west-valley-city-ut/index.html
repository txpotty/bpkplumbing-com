    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in West Valley City

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common causes in local yards.     Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in West Valley City, UT

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a tough crust. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in West Valley City, UT

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor.
