    What Our Gas Leak Repairs Cover in Provo, UT

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Provo, UT

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. If the leak is inside a finished wall, running a new CSST line around it often costs less than opening and patching the wall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Provo, UT

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Provo

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. We start with a gauge on the system to confirm there is a leak at all. Then the sniffer comes out. Our combustible gas detector reads in parts per million, so we can follow the reading fitting by fitting until it peaks. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final answer.

    Permit and Inspection Coordination in Provo

    Replacing more than a connector usually means a permit, and in Provo the inspector will want to see a gauge holding pressure before the pipe is covered. We pull the permit and book the inspection ourselves. Most inspections take under twenty minutes. The test is set to whatever the local fuel gas code requires, often a few psi held for fifteen minutes or longer. The signed permit card stays with your house.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Provo

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. Fence posts and sprinkler trenches hit those lines more often than corrosion does. Dead grass is a tell. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Provo, UT

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Provo

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. We read the tag first, since it lists what the utility found. Then we test everything. After the repair, we run the pressure test the utility or inspector requires and send them the paperwork. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Provo

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. When the leak is small enough, we plan the shutdown for after close. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. Each hose and manifold joint gets tested, and we tag what we isolated so your staff knows what is off. New pipe still needs the commercial sign-off.
