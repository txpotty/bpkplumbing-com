    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Provo

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. Full main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on how deep the pipe is.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these narrow the leak to a small area.

    Many water utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment if you repair the leak quickly and provide proof. We give you an invoice and repair documentation to support your request.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    For underground repairs, we typically use durable materials like PVC, HDPE for trenchless methods or occasionally copper for certain water lines. These materials resist corrosion, root intrusion and ground shifts, providing a long-lasting solution that meets Utah plumbing standards.

    For water lines we typically use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.
