    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Provo, UT

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Mineral deposits reduce the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
