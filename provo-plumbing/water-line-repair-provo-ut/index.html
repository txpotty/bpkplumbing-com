    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Provo, UT

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, and sometimes that buys a few good years. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Provo, UT

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. If it is ours to fix, we fix it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Provo, UT

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Provo

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Fast growth means much of the housing in Utah is relatively new, with PEX supply lines and plastic drains in most recent construction. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Provo

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Provo, UT

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. We answer around the clock. BPK Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, so you are not stuck with a temporary clamp until Monday. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. That happens more than you would think.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Provo, UT

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Provo

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.
