    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Provo, UT

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. Our jetters run around 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe and pushes the loosened grease downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Provo, UT

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. It adds about an hour.

    Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in Provo

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in Provo, UT

    Cost follows trap size and fill level. A 20-gallon under-sink trap is a short visit. Regular pumping keeps the per-visit price down.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Provo, UT

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. Every compartment gets opened and pumped to the bottom. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.

    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Provo

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster. Pumping and hauling are both on us. Everything goes into a sealed tank on the truck, and each load is logged with the gallons and where it went. No barrels left behind.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Provo

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth. Other programs simply set a fixed interval, commonly 90 days for large interceptors. We work to whichever rule applies. Changes are easy.

A hard grease cap gets broken up with a pressure wand before pumping, otherwise it plugs the suction hose and half of it stays in the tank. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.
