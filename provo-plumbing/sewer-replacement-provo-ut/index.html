    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Provo, UT

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions.
