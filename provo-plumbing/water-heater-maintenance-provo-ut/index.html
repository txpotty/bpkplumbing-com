    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Provo

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit.     T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Provo, UT

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Provo

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Provo, UT

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Provo, UT. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Provo, UT

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Provo, UT
