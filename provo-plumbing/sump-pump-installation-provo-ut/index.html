    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Provo

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Provo

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Provo

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Provo, UT

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Provo, UT

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Provo, UT

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Provo

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Provo

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Sump Pump Installation Near Me in Provo, UT

    Local soil and the way Provo, UT homes were built both change how a sump system should go in. BPK Plumbing has set pumps in clay, sand and rock. Clay holds water against the wall longer, which means the pump runs more and needs a bigger pit to rest between cycles. That changes the pump we pick.

    Storm runoff hits every Provo lot a little differently. Before recommending a pump, we walk the yard to see where the downspouts empty, where the grade falls and where the discharge can safely go. The quote comes after that.
