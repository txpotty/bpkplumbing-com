    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Provo, UT

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Provo

    Most households in Provo do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Provo

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. They are popular with families who have small children and people who prefer no open hopper in Provo homes.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Provo, UT

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Provo, UT

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Old gaskets go stiff. We haul the old unit away.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Provo

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Provo homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Provo

    On new builds and remodels in Provo, we coordinate with the general contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together. Plans usually call for a disposal circuit, a drain stub low enough for the trap and a dishwasher hose route with room for a high loop or air gap. Early planning prevents rework.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Provo

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.
