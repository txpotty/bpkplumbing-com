    Pressure Switch Replacement in Provo

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Same-Day Well Pump Repair in Provo

    Hot weather makes a dry house worse. No-water calls get a fast response and, when parts allow, a same-day fix. If a new pump has to be ordered, we can sometimes set up a temporary solution to get the house through. While the truck is on the way, the dispatcher can help you check the breaker and switch.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Provo

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. BPK Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Provo, UT well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Provo

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Provo, UT

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. BPK Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Provo, UT

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and BPK Plumbing services all of them. We work on old standard pumps and newer variable speed drives alike. Common parts are stocked. A pump with life left in it is worth repairing, and that saves the cost of a new motor and the labor to set it.

    Jet Pump Repair in Provo, UT

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Provo restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for air leaks. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Provo

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Provo, UT well.
