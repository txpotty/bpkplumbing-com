    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Provo

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Provo home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Provo, UT

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Summer thunderstorms can bring intense flash floods, especially in canyons and on steep slopes where runoff moves fast. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Provo, UT

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. BPK Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Provo operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.
