    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Provo

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel.     Composite Sink Installation in Provo

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first.     Undermount Sinks on Stone in Provo, UT

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Provo, UT

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop.     Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Provo

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Provo

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first.
