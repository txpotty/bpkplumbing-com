    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Provo, UT

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Provo

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves reaching the valve behind the shower wall.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Provo

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers promptly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Provo

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are simple to clean.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Provo

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause serious damage if left unaddressed. We use modern diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact location of the leak and provide precise repairs, often involving O-ring replacement or tightening connections, preventing costly water damage to your home's structure.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Provo

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Provo

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model.
