    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.
