    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Saratoga Springs, UT homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Saratoga Springs

    Electric water heaters in Saratoga Springs usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.
