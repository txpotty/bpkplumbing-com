    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Saratoga Springs, UT while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.
