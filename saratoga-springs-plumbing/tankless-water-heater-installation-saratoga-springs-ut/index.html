    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Saratoga Springs

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line often needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and haul away your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Yes, installing a new tankless water heater or replacing an existing one in Saratoga Springs typically requires a plumbing permit. This ensures the installation meets local building and safety codes. BPK Plumbing handles all necessary permitting paperwork for you.

    We install leading tankless water heater brands known for their reliability and efficiency, including Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem. Our plumbers are trained and experienced with the particular requirements of each manufacturer to deliver strong performance.

    Yes, gas tankless water heaters require specific venting materials and configurations different from traditional tank heaters.     A standard tankless water heater installation, replacing an existing tank, usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If extensive plumbing or gas line changes are required, it may take a full day. Our team works efficiently to restore your hot water as quickly as possible.

    Tankless water heaters offer several key benefits: endless hot water on demand, real energy savings from heating water only when needed, a longer lifespan (often 20+ years) and a compact, space-saving design. They also lower the risk of tank leaks and the water damage that comes with them.
