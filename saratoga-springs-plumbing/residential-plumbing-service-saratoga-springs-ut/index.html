    Water Softener and Filtration Service in Saratoga Springs

    Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. We program regeneration to your household's use.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs more involved, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Pipe Repair and Repipes in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. A single leak gets a clean copper splice or a PEX transition. Repeat leaks mean a repipe. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. Our techs work on Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai heaters, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. Old tanks get hauled off.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Saratoga Springs

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. Warm spots on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off or a water bill that doubles are the usual clues. Nothing gets quoted until the leak is pinpointed. Fixes range from a direct repair to rerouting the line overhead or a full repipe when leaks keep coming back.

    Gas Line Repair and Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us. We install new gas lines in black iron or CSST to ranges, dryers and outdoor grills, then pressure-test the line and check every joint with a leak detector before the appliance gets lit. Gas work gets a permit wherever the local code asks for one.

    Water Heater Service and Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT
