    Root Intrusion Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Soils in Utah vary from clay that swells when wet to rocky benches and collapsible soils that settle when they take on water. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.
