    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Saratoga Springs, UT

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs slower, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
