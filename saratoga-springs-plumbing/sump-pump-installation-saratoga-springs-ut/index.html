    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Saratoga Springs

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Saratoga Springs

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Saratoga Springs

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Saratoga Springs

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Local Sump Pump Installers in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Local soil and the way Saratoga Springs, UT homes were built both change how a sump system should go in. BPK Plumbing has dug pits in every kind of ground around here. In clay the water stays against the foundation longer, so the pump runs more often and a larger basin helps the motor last. That changes the pump we pick.
