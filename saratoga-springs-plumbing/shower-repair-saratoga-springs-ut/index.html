    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Saratoga Springs

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Shower Diverter Repair in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Saratoga Springs

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.
