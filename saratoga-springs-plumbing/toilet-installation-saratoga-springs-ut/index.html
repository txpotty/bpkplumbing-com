    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Smart Toilet Installation in Saratoga Springs, UT

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord.     Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Saratoga Springs

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.
