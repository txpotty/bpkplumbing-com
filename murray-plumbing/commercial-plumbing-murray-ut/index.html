    Backflow Testing and Certification in Murray

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Murray

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Murray

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers are routine work for us. We also handle ADA upgrades. For ADA remodels we set fixture heights, add grab bar blocking in the wall and insulate the drain and supply lines under sinks.

    Grease Trap Installation in Murray

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Murray, UT

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.
