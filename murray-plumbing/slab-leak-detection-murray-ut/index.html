    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Murray, UT

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Murray.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Murray

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Murray

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Murray homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Murray, UT

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Murray, UT

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Murray home.
