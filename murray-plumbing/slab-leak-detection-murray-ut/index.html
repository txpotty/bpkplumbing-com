    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Murray

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Urgent Slab Leak Calls in Murray, UT

    A slab leak that is soaking carpet or pushing water up through tile cannot wait until next week. Shut off the main. BPK Plumbing sends a tech to Murray, UT homes quickly, isolates the failed side and gets the rest of the house back on water while the repair gets planned.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Murray, UT

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Murray, UT home.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Murray, UT

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Murray, UT

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Murray, UT

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, so we run a camera through the line to find cracks, separated joints and bellies. You see the footage yourself before any repair talk starts in your Murray home.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Murray, UT

    Large open floors can hide a leak. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, so we can rule out half the house before setting up the microphone. It saves an hour on large homes across Murray, UT.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Murray

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.
