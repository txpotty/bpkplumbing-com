    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Murray

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until serious damage has occurred.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Murray, UT

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    In most communities the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner is in charge of the service line from the meter to the house. We confirm which side the leak is on before quoting you.

    Water lines can leak or break due to several factors, including aging pipes (corrosion in galvanized steel or copper), shifting soil, tree root intrusion, high water pressure, ground freezing and thawing cycles, and accidental damage during excavation.     Most standard water line repairs can be completed within a few hours to a full day, depending on the severity and accessibility of the leak.
