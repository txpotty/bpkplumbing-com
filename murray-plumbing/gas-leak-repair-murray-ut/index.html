    The cost of gas leak repair in Murray, UT can vary significantly depending on the leak's location, severity and the extent of pipe damage. We provide a clear estimate after a thorough inspection.

    Hissing near gas lines, dead patches of landscaping over a buried line, bubbles in standing water and higher gas bills can all point to a leak. Some people also notice headaches or dizziness.

    We use quality, code-compliant gas line components from reputable manufacturers like TracPipe, Gastite and NIBCO. Our focus is on durable, reliable parts that protect the long-term safety and integrity of your gas system.

    A carbon monoxide detector does not detect natural gas leaks. Natural gas has no smell of its own, so an additive gives it a rotten egg odor, while carbon monoxide is an odorless, colorless gas that results from incomplete combustion. While a carbon monoxide detector is essential for safety, it won't alert you to a gas leak. You need to rely on your sense of smell or a separate gas leak detector.

    The most common signs of a gas leak include a distinct smell of rotten eggs or sulfur, a hissing or whistling sound near a gas line, visible damage to a gas pipe or dead vegetation over an underground gas line. Indoor symptoms might also include dizziness, nausea or fatigue. If you notice any of these, evacuate and call for help immediately.

    The duration of a gas leak repair varies greatly. Simple repairs might take an hour or two, while complex issues involving excavation or extensive pipe replacement could take a full day or more. We aim for prompt service while putting safety and thoroughness first.

    For significant gas line repairs or replacements in Utah, particularly those involving altering or adding to the existing system, permits from local building authorities are typically required.     Most areas have no annual requirement for homeowner gas line inspections, but it is strongly recommended to have your gas lines inspected every 3 to 5 years, or whenever you notice a potential issue like an odd smell or a drop in appliance performance.
