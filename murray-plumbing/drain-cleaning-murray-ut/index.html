    A kitchen or bathroom sink usually takes 1 to 2 hours, including setup and cleanup. Mains add time. A main line blockage or a jetting job can run 2 to 4 hours, depending on access and how much buildup is in the pipe.

    Our trucks carry RIDGID, Spartan Tool and Gorlitz machines. Those brands hold up in daily use. Each one comes with cutter heads sized for different pipe, from small fixture lines up to 6 inch commercial mains.

    Water backing up into a different fixture is the giveaway. That means a shared line is blocked. A single slow sink is usually a trap or a short branch, but a toilet that bubbles when the tub drains means the blockage is farther down.

    Slow drains are the first sign. Gurgling pipes and a sewer smell come next. Standing water in a tub or water coming up in one fixture when another drains means the clog is past the trap and into a shared line, which is the point where a plunger will not help.
