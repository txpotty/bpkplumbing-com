    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Murray

    The cost for a gas water heater installation in Murray usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,000, depending on the unit size, type (tank vs. tankless) and any necessary gas line or venting modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a site assessment.

    In most areas, a permit is generally required for gas water heater installation to confirm compliance with local building and plumbing codes. Utah adopts the International Plumbing Code with state amendments, and plumbers must be licensed by the state to do the work. BPK Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications in Murray and makes sure your installation passes inspection.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 and 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or major gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow more flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the smarter option.
