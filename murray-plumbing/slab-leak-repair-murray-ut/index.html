    Finding and Fixing Slab Leaks in Murray, UT

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Murray

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Murray home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Murray, UT

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Murray, UT

    A spot repair costs least up front. Then add the flooring. Reroutes cost somewhat more but skip the concrete work and prevent future leaks in that line, and a whole-house repipe makes sense once you have had several slab leaks or have old copper throughout. You get clear prices for each.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Murray, UT

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Slab Leak Detection in Murray

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Murray

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Murray

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Murray homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Murray

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Murray homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Local Slab Leak Repair in Murray, UT

    Local plumbing has local habits. Each era of homes was plumbed its own way. We know which subdivisions used copper under the slab and which used PEX, so we can guess the failure before testing starts. That is local Murray knowledge.

    We know the slabs and soil common in this area. That shapes the repair. Older slabs without tendons can be cut where needed, while post-tension slabs often make a reroute the better choice. Detection usually takes one to three hours.
