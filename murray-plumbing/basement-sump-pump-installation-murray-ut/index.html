    The Sump Pump Work We Do in Murray, UT

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Murray

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Murray homes. Expect more noise.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Systems in Murray, UT

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. We use a stopwatch.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Murray

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid. A submersible with a gasketed cover keeps the noise down, and we route the discharge behind framing or through a closet so the pipe does not run across a wall you just painted in your Murray home. The concrete patch ends up flush with the floor.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Murray

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. Our plumbers size the unit to the water coming in and the height it has to climb, not to the sale sticker. In Murray, we set the basin at the low corner, bed it in washed gravel and run 1-1/2 inch PVC out through the rim joist. Discharge gets carried far enough out that it cannot soak back toward the wall.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Murray

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. We add a spring check valve so the water in the pipe cannot fall back, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. We fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Sump Pit Installation in Murray, UT

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Less cycling, longer life.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Murray, UT

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Spring snowmelt can raise groundwater and creek levels in Utah, sometimes pushing water into basements that stay dry the rest of the year. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Same-Day Sump Pump Installation in Murray

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm. We remove standing water from the pit first, then set a new pump on a new check valve, plug it into a tested GFCI outlet and watch it run through several cycles before leaving your Murray basement. A backup is the next conversation.
