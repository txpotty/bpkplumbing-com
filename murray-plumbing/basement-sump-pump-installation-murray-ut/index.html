    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Murray, UT

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We offer and install battery backup sump pump systems that switch on automatically if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Murray, UT. Summer thunderstorms can bring intense flash floods, especially in canyons and on steep slopes where runoff moves fast. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Solutions in Murray, UT

    For homes with significant groundwater issues or large basements, a standard sump pump may not be enough. BPK Plumbing installs high-capacity sump pump systems designed to move larger volumes of water quickly and efficiently. These systems often feature more powerful motors and larger discharge lines. We conduct a thorough assessment of your property to recommend and install a system that can handle even the heaviest rainfall.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Murray

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Murray

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Frost-free hose bibs and well insulated pipes are standard practice in Utah, especially in mountain areas with deep, long cold. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Murray

    The cost of basement sump pump installation in Murray usually ranges from $800 to $2,000, depending on the type of pump, the complexity of the installation and whether a new sump pit needs to be dug. Battery backup systems or long discharge line runs can raise the final price. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after an on-site assessment.

    Replacing a pump generally does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they apply.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also warning signs.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is usually not allowed by local codes.

    We regularly install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne because they offer cast iron housings, reliable switches and good parts support. We will match the brand and model to your pit size and budget.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, designed to handle everyday water removal. A secondary sump pump, often a battery-powered backup, serves as an emergency system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or is overwhelmed by excess water. Both work together for the best protection.

    BPK Plumbing offers a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will give you all warranty details at the time of installation.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit usually takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching usually takes most of a day.
