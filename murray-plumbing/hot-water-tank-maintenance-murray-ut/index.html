    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Murray

    The cost of hot water tank maintenance in Murray, UT generally ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required. This normally includes flushing, anode rod inspection and safety valve checks. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    Once a year is the baseline for most homes, and more often where water is hard. Homes without a softener or with heavy use may benefit from a flush every six months.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, odd noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These point to issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves professional tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to ensure safety and code compliance.

    Many homeowners can do a basic drain with a garden hose. The risk is a plastic drain valve that breaks or will not reseal, and DIY flushes seldom include an anode check or relief valve test.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to inefficient operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service prevents these costly and dangerous issues.

    Regular, professional maintenance can frequently be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but in most cases, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.
