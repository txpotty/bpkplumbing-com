    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Murray

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Murray

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. The first sign is often a spot. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, so we locate them with a thermal camera and moisture meter before cutting a single opening in the wall or ceiling. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Murray, UT

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Crusty fittings are a clue. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. The joint gets tested.
