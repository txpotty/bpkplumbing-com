    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Murray, UT

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Apartment buildings, hotels and offices see heavy daily flow. Commercial stacks and mains get jetted outside business hours, with a written report for the manager.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Murray

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Murray, UT

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Murray

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. Downtime costs them money. We schedule around slow hours and keep video records for property managers.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Murray, UT

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Main Line Jetting in Murray, UT

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Murray, UT

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Murray

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.
