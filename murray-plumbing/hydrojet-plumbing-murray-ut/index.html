    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Murray, UT

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Murray, UT provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it usually leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, by contrast, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more lasting solution to your drainage issues.

    Root Removal Jetting in Murray

    Roots from thirsty shade trees and shrubs seek out small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root-cutting nozzle chops and flushes the root mass, while high pressure clears the fine hairs left behind. Jetting is a strong maintenance tool, but roots will return if the joint is broken. We will show you the entry point and discuss lining or repair when it makes sense.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Murray, UT

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 including camera inspection. Commercial lines and long runs can cost $800 to $2,000 or more. Jetting costs more up front, but it often prevents repeat calls that add up over a year. We help you decide which method suits your line and budget.
