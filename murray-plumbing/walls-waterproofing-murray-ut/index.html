    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Murray

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Murray, UT can vary a lot, typically ranging from $3,000 for interior crack sealing to $15,000 or more for extensive exterior foundation waterproofing with drainage systems. Factors like the size of the area, severity of water damage, chosen methods, and accessibility all affect the final price. We provide detailed, upfront quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Damp proofing is a less robust solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems built to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is the right choice.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty in force.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is the smart choice.

    BPK Plumbing works with trusted manufacturers known for high-quality waterproofing products. We commonly use products from brands like Xypex, Tremco, and Sika for their proven effectiveness and durability, from crystalline admixtures to elastomeric membranes and sealants. We select the best product for your foundation type and site.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for many decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for long-term durability.

    Look for signs such as damp or wet spots on basement or crawl space walls, a musty odor, visible mold or mildew growth, efflorescence, a white powdery residue, on concrete or brick, and standing water.
