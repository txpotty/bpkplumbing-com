    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Murray, UT

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in Murray

    Some lines need more than a cable. That is jetting work. Water at up to 4,000 PSI scours grease, scale, mineral deposits and roots off the pipe wall, cleaning the full diameter instead of punching a hole through the blockage.

    Residential Sewer Line Cleaning in Murray

    Household laterals collect grease and hair. It takes months to close a line. Kitchen grease and bathroom hair stick to rough spots and joints until the 4 inch line narrows enough to back up, and older cast iron makes it worse by adding scale for debris to grab, and we clear it with a cable or a jetter sized for the pipe.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Murray

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Murray

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Murray, UT

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Digging costs far more. A sound pipe is worth maintaining, while a collapsed or sagging one may need repair, and the camera tells us which, and you see both prices.
