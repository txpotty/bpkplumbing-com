    Venting and Draft Repair in Murray, UT

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Murray

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Murray are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Murray, UT

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800.     Gas Control Valve Replacement in Murray, UT

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Murray

    A common culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a malfunctioning igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to ensure a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Murray

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Murray, UT

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these crucial components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats efficiently and safely.
