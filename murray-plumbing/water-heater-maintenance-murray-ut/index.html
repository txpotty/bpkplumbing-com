    Tank Flush and Inspection in Murray

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. The tank heats better. So does recovery time.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Murray, UT

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Murray

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Murray, UT

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. High demand speeds up scale and wear, so commercial units need service on a tighter schedule. Our techs know these systems. Hot water stays on for your business.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Murray

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Takes about an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Murray, UT
