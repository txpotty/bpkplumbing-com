    Resin Testing and Replacement in Murray

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Murray

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly.     Resin Bed Replacement in Murray

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. BPK Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Murray

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Murray

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Murray, UT

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.
