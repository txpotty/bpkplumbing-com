    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Murray

    Most basic drain clearing in Murray, UT runs $150 to $500. A main line costs more than a sink. Jetting costs more than cabling, and sewer repairs run from a few thousand for a spot fix to much more for replacement.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. Jetters are trailer or cart units. A locating camera pinpoints the problem, so a repair means one small hole, not a long trench.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Clay pipe can split over time. A root mass can also push a joint out of line, creating an offset that catches waste, and in bad cases the pipe collapses and has to be dug up or lined from the inside.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. That usually means the main line. In the yard, water that stands for a day or more after rain is the cue.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    Store-bought cleaners rarely clear a real clog. They can make things worse. The chemicals sit in the trap, heat up and can damage older pipe or soften glue joints, and if the clog does not clear, the plumber now has to open a trap full of caustic liquid. A hand auger is the better tool.

    Installations like French drains, dry wells and sewer repairs carry a workmanship warranty. Drain clearing has a separate guarantee. The terms are written on your invoice, and if a yard drain we installed does not move water the way we said it would, we come back and correct it at no charge for labor.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron housings hold up best. For homes that lose power in storms, we add a battery backup on its own float and a high-water alarm, so a dead primary pump or a power outage does not turn into a flooded floor.
