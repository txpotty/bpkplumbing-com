    Long-Lasting Whole-Home Repipe Services in Murray, UT

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Murray, UT

    Many older homes in Murray, UT still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Murray, UT

    Mineral deposits reduce the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Murray

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Murray

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Murray

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where leaks can go unnoticed. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Cost-Effective Repiping Comparisons in Murray, UT

    Understanding the cost of repiping is crucial for homeowners in Murray, UT. We provide transparent comparisons between PEX and copper repiping options, detailing material costs, labor, and expected lifespan. PEX is generally more affordable for materials and installation, while copper offers a premium, long-lasting solution with excellent resale value. Our estimates include all necessary permits, fittings, and cleanup, ensuring you have a clear picture of your investment before any work begins, with no hidden fees.

    Copper Repiping in Murray, UT

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Murray, UT

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Murray, UT has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. BPK Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a dependable new system.
