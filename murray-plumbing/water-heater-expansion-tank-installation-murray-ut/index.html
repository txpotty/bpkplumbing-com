    Brands and Tank Materials in Murray, UT

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Stainless steel connections resist corrosion from hard or aggressive water. We avoid tanks designed only for hydronic heating, since they are not approved for drinking water.

    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Murray

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you stable pressure throughout the house.

    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Murray

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Expansion Tank for Tankless Water Heaters in Murray, UT

    While tankless water heaters do not store hot water, an expansion tank can still be beneficial, and sometimes required, in Murray, UT plumbing systems. If your tankless unit is part of a closed system, an expansion tank can help absorb pressure spikes from the cold water supply or reduce wear on components. Our plumbers assess your tankless setup and local codes to determine whether an expansion tank is necessary and recommend the ideal solution for your system's longevity and performance.

    Expansion Tank Installation Cost Comparison in Murray, UT

    Adding an expansion tank during a water heater replacement is the most cost-effective approach because the water is already off and the piping is open. Installing one as a standalone job costs a bit more in labor but still pays off by protecting the heater. Larger tanks, attic installs and PRV replacement add to the total. We provide a clear price before any work begins.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Expansion Tanks in Murray

    Apartment buildings, restaurants and offices with large commercial water heaters or multiple tanks need larger expansion tanks sized for total system volume. We calculate requirements for the entire hot water system, including any storage tanks and recirculation loops. Larger tanks are floor-mounted with proper supports.
