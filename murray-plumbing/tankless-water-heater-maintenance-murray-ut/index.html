    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Murray

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Murray, UT generally ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required. This includes a full flush and inspection of critical components. More complex issues or additional repairs would be quoted on their own.

    Rinnai shows LC codes, Navien may show E003 or E030 and Noritz often shows 90 or 99 when scale or airflow is a problem. These codes typically clear after proper cleaning.

    If you have isolation valves, a pump and the right solution, a handy homeowner can do it. A pro visit also checks gas pressure, venting, sensors and condensate, which a DIY flush leaves out.

    Once a year is standard, and homes with very hard water and no softener may need it every six to nine months. Heavy users and commercial units need it more frequently.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, improving heat transfer efficiency.

    A cold water sandwich can come from restricted flow, a dirty inlet filter or a flow sensor not reading correctly. Descaling and filter cleaning often improve it.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can void the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your own warranty terms.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, unusual noises coming from the unit, or error codes displayed on the control panel. If you notice any of these, it's a strong indication that your tankless water heater in Murray is due for professional maintenance.
