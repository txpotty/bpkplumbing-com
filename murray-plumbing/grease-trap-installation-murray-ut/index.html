    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Murray

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. BPK Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Murray for the best efficiency.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Murray, UT
