    Commercial Disposal Setup in Murray

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Murray

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Murray

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink.     Standard Disposal Installation in Murray

    Most households in Murray do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit.
