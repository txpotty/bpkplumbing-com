    What We Replace on Well Systems in Murray, UT

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Murray

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Murray, UT

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Murray

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Well Pump Sizing in Murray

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Murray

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Murray

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. We check it on every pump job.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Murray, UT

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    No-Water Calls in Murray

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. We test the control box and pressure switch before pulling anything.
