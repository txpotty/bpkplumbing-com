    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Murray, UT Home

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Murray, UT

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Murray

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Murray

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Murray, UT

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Check the breaker and the GFCI outlet first, then lift the lid and see whether the float is stuck. A wet vac buys time. Our trucks carry common sizes, so most emergency calls end with a new pump running the same visit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Murray, UT

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Murray

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Murray

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Murray

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.
