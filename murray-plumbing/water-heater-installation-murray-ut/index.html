    Electric water heaters are common in homes without natural gas access or where homeowners prefer electric appliances.     Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Murray

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Utah adopts the International Plumbing Code with state amendments, and plumbers must be licensed by the state to do the work. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Murray. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Murray, UT

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Murray, UT

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Murray, UT

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in Utah homes. Our experts specialize in installing these highly efficient units, making sure of proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed.
