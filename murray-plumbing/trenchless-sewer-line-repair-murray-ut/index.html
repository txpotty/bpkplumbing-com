    Root Intrusion Repair in Murray, UT

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Murray, UT

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs more involved, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Services in Murray

    A sudden collapse usually shows up as every drain in the house backing up at once, often into the lowest tub or floor drain. First we restore flow. Then the camera shows whether the line can be lined or needs a burst. Some collapses need a small dig.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Murray, UT

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Work in Murray, UT

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Murray

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet.     Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Murray

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.
