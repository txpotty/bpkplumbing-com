    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Murray

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Pull-down hoses crack at the spot where they flex over the neck, and the leak runs inside the body down into the cabinet where it stays hidden.     Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Murray, UT

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. The mounting nuts sit behind the bowl in a space about the width of a hand, and after twenty years they are usually crusted to the shank.     Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Replacement in Murray, UT
