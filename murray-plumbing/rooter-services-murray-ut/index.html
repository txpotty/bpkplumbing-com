    Rooting vs. Pipe Repair in Murray, UT

    Rooting is the right fix when the pipe is sound but clogged. If roots keep returning quickly, or the cable meets broken pipe, a repair may save money long term. Rooting usually costs a few hundred dollars, while lining or replacement costs more but ends repeat clogs. A camera inspection shows which option fits best.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Murray, UT

    For the most stubborn clogs and extensive pipe buildup, BPK Plumbing offers hydro-jetting services in Murray, UT. This technique uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of your pipes, removing grease, mineral deposits, and even tree roots. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Hydro-jetting cleans pipes more thoroughly than traditional snaking, restoring them to near-original condition and preventing future clogs.

    Rooter Plus Hydro Jetting in Murray

    Rooting cuts through a clog, while jetting scours the inside of the pipe. Combining the two clears heavy roots and grease more thoroughly than either one by itself. We camera the line before jetting to make sure the pipe can handle the pressure. This combination is a common choice for restaurants and homes with recurring root problems.
