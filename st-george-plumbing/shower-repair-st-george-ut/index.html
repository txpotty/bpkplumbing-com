    Shower Handle Leak Repair in St. George

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in St. George, UT

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in St. George, UT

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Diverter Repair in St. George, UT

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in St. George

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then call us. Our plumber locates the leak, stops it and starts drying the area.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in St. George

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in St. George

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in St. George

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.
