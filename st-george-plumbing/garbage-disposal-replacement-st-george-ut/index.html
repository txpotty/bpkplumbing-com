    InSinkErator Replacement in St. George

    Most InSinkErator units share the same Quick Lock mount, so a like-for-like swap can go fast when the old mount is in good shape.     Standard Garbage Disposal Replacement in St. George

    Every swap starts with the power off. We disconnect the dishwasher hose and the discharge tube, turn the mounting ring to drop the old unit, then pull the old flange and clean off the dried putty so the new flange seats flat in the sink opening. Fresh putty, new gaskets and a new mount go in. Every joint gets checked with water running before we finish.

    Like-for-Like Disposal Swap in St. George

    If the replacement uses the same mount as the old unit, the swap is quick and the trap usually lines up without changes. We still replace the flange gasket and putty if they have hardened, and we check the discharge tube for cracks, since old plastic tubes split right at the gasket. Figure on an hour. The old unit leaves with us.

    Waste King and Moen Replacement in St. George, UT

    Waste King uses the EZ Mount system, which twists on by hand, and many Moen units use a similar style. We match the replacement to the mount when it makes sense, or replace the flange and mount when switching brands, so the new disposal hangs tight with no vibration at the sink. Switching brands adds some time. The flange has to come out.

    InSinkErator Model Upgrades in St. George

    Badger to Evolution is the most common upgrade we do, since the Evolution line adds grind stages and sound insulation. Because both lines use the same Quick Lock mount, the swap often keeps the existing flange and ring if they are in good condition, which saves time. Evolution units are taller. We measure first.
