    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathroom Plumbing in St. George

    The cost of bathroom plumbing repair in St. George can vary widely depending on the issue.     Replacing a standard shower head is often a straightforward DIY task. However, if you are experiencing low water pressure, leaks from the wall, or plan to install a complex multi-function shower system, it is best to call a professional for proper installation and to prevent leaks.

    The lifespan of bathroom plumbing fixtures varies, with toilets lasting 20 to 50 years, faucets 15 to 20 years, and shower valves 20 to 30 years. However, factors like water quality, frequency of use, and maintenance can affect these timelines. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Harder water can sometimes lead to earlier wear on internal components.

    To prevent clogs, avoid flushing anything but toilet paper down the toilet. For sinks and showers, use drain strainers to catch hair and soap scum. Now and then flush drains with hot water, and avoid pouring grease or harsh chemicals down them. Regular preventative maintenance from BPK Plumbing can also help keep your drains clear.

    Minor repairs like replacing a faucet or toilet typically do not require a permit. Water heater replacements, gas line work and new bathroom plumbing typically need a permit and an inspection from the local building department.
