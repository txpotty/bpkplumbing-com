    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in St. George, UT

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     Preventive Care and Longevity in St. George, UT

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Fibrous scraps jam it most.     After-Hours Disposal Service in St. George

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Skip the drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in St. George, UT

    A disposal repair that costs half of a new unit rarely makes sense, and we will say so. Many fixes are cheap. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in St. George, UT

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Leak Detection and Repair in St. George, UT

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in St. George

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.
