    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in St. George

    Residential storm drain cleaning in St. George usually runs $200 to $500 depending on the number of inlets and pipe length. Dry well cleaning and commercial systems typically cost more and are quoted after a site visit.

    You can clear grates and remove surface debris with a scoop. Pipes packed with silt and gravel typically need jetting and vacuum equipment.

    To prevent storm drain clogs, regularly clear leaves and debris from grates and catch basins, avoid planting trees directly over drain lines, and keep landscaping in good shape to prevent soil erosion. Consider installing grates with smaller openings in high-debris areas and schedule yearly professional cleanings.

    Cleaning does not need a permit. New dry wells or major drainage changes may require permits or registration, and we can help you.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is highly effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    Common signs include water pooling around your property after rain, slow drainage from downspouts, visible debris collecting at catch basin grates, and unusual odors coming from the drains. If you notice any of these, it is a good idea to have your storm drains inspected.

    We use a range of professional-grade equipment, including heavy-duty hydro jetting machines that use high-pressure water to scour pipes, drain snakes with various cutting heads for root removal, and waterproof camera inspection systems to accurately diagnose issues and confirm thorough cleaning.
