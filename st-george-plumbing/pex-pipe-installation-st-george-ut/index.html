    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in St. George

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in St. George can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in St. George, UT

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in St. George

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in St. George typically takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers commonly offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and failed inspections.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many St. George homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older St. George homes and point to a failing plumbing system.
