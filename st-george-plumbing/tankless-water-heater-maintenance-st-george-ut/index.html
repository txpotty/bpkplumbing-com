    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in St. George, UT

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in St. George

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in St. George

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in St. George, UT

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. A descaling visit costs a fraction of that. Cared for, many units run 15 to 20 years. We will also say whether a softener or a scale inhibitor makes sense for your water.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in St. George, UT

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle. Inlet screens and air filters get pulled and cleaned. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in St. George, UT

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in St. George, UT

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in St. George, UT

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around St. George, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. Warranty claims later can hinge on it.
