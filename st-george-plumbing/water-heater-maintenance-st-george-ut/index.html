    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in St. George

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. Pans get inspected. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in St. George

    A blue flame is what we want to see.     Water Heater Leak Checks in St. George, UT

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in St. George, UT

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Tankless Descaling in St. George, UT

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. We clean the inlet filter. We also check the burner and venting.

Spent rods get replaced. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.
