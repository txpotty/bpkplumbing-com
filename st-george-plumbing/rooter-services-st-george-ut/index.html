    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in St. George

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners, especially for stubborn clogs. These products can damage pipes over time, especially older ones common in St. George's older neighborhoods, and often only provide a temporary fix. Professional rooter services are safer and more effective for a lasting fix.

    No permit is needed for routine rooting. Installing a new cleanout or repairing pipe may require one. We handle permits for any repair work.

    We use top-quality equipment for our rooter services. Our technicians commonly use professional-grade tools from brands like RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz for drain augers, sewer cameras, and hydro-jetting machines. These brands are known for their durability and performance in tough plumbing situations.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera inspection can help set the best schedule.

    Tree roots are naturally attracted to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a common issue in St. George neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for efficient service with lasting results.
