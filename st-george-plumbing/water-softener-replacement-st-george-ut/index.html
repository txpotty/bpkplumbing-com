    Metered Softener Replacement in St. George

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Emergency Water Softener Failure Replacement in St. George, UT

    A sudden failure of your water softener can lead to instant hard water problems, affecting laundry, dishes and even skin. If your unit is leaking, not regenerating or showing error codes, BPK Plumbing offers emergency replacement services in St. George, UT.     Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in St. George, UT

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They need no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in St. George

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in St. George. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Brine Tank Replacement in St. George

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.
