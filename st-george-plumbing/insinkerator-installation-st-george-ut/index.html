    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in St. George

    Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.

    Common signs you need a new garbage disposal include frequent clogs, persistent leaks from the unit, unusual loud noises during operation, or a complete failure to turn on. If your unit constantly jams or gives off a foul odor even after cleaning, it might be time for a replacement.

    Most standard Insinkerator installations take between 1 to 2 hours. If there are complications, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, it might take a bit longer. We aim for efficiency without compromising quality.
