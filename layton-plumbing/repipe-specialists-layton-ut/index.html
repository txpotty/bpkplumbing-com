    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Layton

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Copper Repiping in Layton, UT

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Layton

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Layton. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Layton, UT

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. BPK Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Layton, UT, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Layton

    Most PEX repipes for Utah homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes typically run 20 to 40 percent more.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a strong choice for hard water. Copper is durable too but costs more and can pinhole in aggressive water.

    We primarily install high-quality copper piping from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro, known for their durability and purity. For PEX systems, we often use leading brands such as Uponor (Wirsbo Aquapex), Zurn PEX, or Viega PEX, chosen for their proven reliability and performance in homes.

    Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup restricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can mitigate these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    A whole-home repipe requires permits, pressure testing and code knowledge. Most homeowners are better served by a licensed crew that can finish fast and pass inspection.

    In most cases, yes, you can stay in your home during the repiping process. Our team works to minimize disruption and will typically make sure you have water service at the end of each day. We take care to protect your property and keep the work area clean and safe throughout the job.
