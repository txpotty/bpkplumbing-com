    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Layton, UT

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Layton, UT

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Layton

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Layton, UT

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. Exterior block sealing depends on wall length. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Layton

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs slower, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Layton

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes listed for continuous wet use, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. We can work after hours.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Layton, UT

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Higher elevations in and around Layton bring colder nights and longer winters, which puts extra load on water heaters and heating systems. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Layton, UT

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes from the 1980s and 1990s sometimes have polybutylene piping, which is prone to failure and a common reason to repipe. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.
