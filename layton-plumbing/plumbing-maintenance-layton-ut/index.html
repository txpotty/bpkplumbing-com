    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Layton

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Scheduled Service for Businesses in Layton

    Commercial plumbing takes a beating. We service large water heaters, restroom fixtures, backflow preventers and kitchen drain lines on a set schedule, so failures are caught on a quiet Tuesday morning instead of a busy Friday night. Inspections go smoother. You get records for every visit.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Layton, UT

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Layton

    Sewer problems hide underground. The camera finds roots, grease, sags and offsets before they back up into the house. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where slow leaks can go unnoticed. We show you the video. Catching a cracked joint early can mean a spot repair instead of a full replacement.

    Sump Pump Testing in Layton, UT

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Spring snowmelt can raise groundwater and creek levels in Utah, sometimes pushing water into basements that stay dry the rest of the year. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Battery backups get load tested.

    Water Softener Service in Layton

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Layton

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Layton

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Older resin may need replacing.
