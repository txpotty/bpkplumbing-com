    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Layton

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Layton

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Layton

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Layton

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Layton

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Layton

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Layton, UT
