    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Layton, UT

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Layton, UT

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. Air pressure forces water to the fixtures. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, which waterlogs the tank. Replacement is the fix.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Layton

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.
