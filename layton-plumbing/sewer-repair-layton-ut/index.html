    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather.     Root Intrusion Removal in Layton, UT

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Layton neighborhoods.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Layton, UT

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Layton, UT

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Layton.
