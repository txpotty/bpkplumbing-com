    Softener Systems We Replace in Layton, UT

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Layton, UT

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Layton

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Layton, UT

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Layton

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Layton

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Softener Loop Installation in Layton, UT

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Layton, UT

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Utah homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. The drain line gets an air gap.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Layton

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.
