    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Layton

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Layton

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. Starved units throw error codes.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Layton, UT

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Most homeowners here blow out sprinkler systems each fall and shut off outdoor spigots to keep lines from cracking. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Layton

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. The unit is chosen from that figure. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Layton, UT

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Layton, UT

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Layton, UT

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Layton, UT

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.
