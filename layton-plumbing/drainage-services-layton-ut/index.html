    Hydro-Jetting Services in Layton

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Grease lines benefit most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    Drainage System Maintenance in Layton

    Maintenance on a drain system costs less than one emergency visit. For homes with a history of root intrusion, we cut roots from the sewer every 12 to 18 months and camera the line so we can see if a section needs repair before it collapses. Yard drains get flushed too.

    Restaurant and Industrial Drain Service in Layton

    Restaurants and industrial buildings send heavier loads down their drains than homes do. Interceptors need regular pumping. Kitchen lines get jetted between pump-outs, and we keep a service log for your inspector.

    French Drain Installation in Layton

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. Filter fabric around the stone keeps silt out. It needs steady fall to an outlet, and the outlet gets a grate to keep critters out.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Layton, UT

    Several drains backing up at once points to the main line, not a single fixture. Clearing runs from the outdoor cleanout with a root head, followed by a camera run to the street. No cleanout? Adding one makes every future call cheaper.

    Sump Pump Services in Layton, UT

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater. We test the float, check the discharge line and check valve, and replace pumps that hum, run nonstop, or cannot keep up during a hard rain. Never pipe it to the sewer. Many sump floods come from a blocked discharge line, not a dead pump.

    Jetting for Scale and Grease in Layton

    Jetting uses water at high pressure to strip a pipe back to its original diameter.
