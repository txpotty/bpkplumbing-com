    Kitchen Line Grease Jetting in Layton, UT

    Kitchen drains are the most common grease trouble spot in any home or restaurant. Grease clings to pipe walls and hardens, especially where the line runs flat under a slab. Jetting melts and scrubs this buildup away instead of just poking a hole through it. We use smaller hose and nozzles sized for 1-1/2 and 2 inch kitchen lines. This keeps your sink and dishwasher draining properly for much longer.

    Residential Hydro Jetting in Layton

    For homeowners in Layton facing recurring clogs, slow drains, or foul odors, residential hydro jetting is a powerful solution. Our service targets common household causes like grease buildup from kitchens, hair and soap scum from bathrooms, and even laundry detergent residue. We use specialized nozzles and adjustable pressure to safely clear blockages in pipes ranging from small kitchen lines to main sewer connections, restoring optimal drainage and preventing future issues.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Layton

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and bring back flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond properly.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Layton, UT

    Cured-in-place pipe lining needs a clean surface to adhere and seal correctly. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready. Any heavy obstructions are cut out before the liner is installed. Skipping this step can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding. Proper prep is part of why lined pipe can last for decades.

    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Layton

    High-pressure water jetting, or hydro jetting, is a non-invasive method that uses strongly pressurized water to clear and clean pipes. Our equipment in Layton can generate water pressures up to 4,000 PSI, powerful enough to break apart stubborn blockages and scour pipe walls clean without chemicals. This method is effective for many pipe materials, from PVC to cast iron, and is an environmentally friendly choice for deep drain cleaning.

    Preventive Maintenance Jetting in Layton

    If your line has a history of clogs, a maintenance plan can keep it clear. We schedule jetting every 12 to 24 months for homes and more often for busy commercial kitchens. Regular cleaning prevents the buildup from ever reaching the point of a backup. It also gives us a chance to camera the line and spot new problems early. Maintenance customers get priority scheduling.

    Sewer Line Descaling with Hydro Jetting in Layton

    Over time, mineral deposits and other materials can build up on the interior walls of sewer lines, forming a tough scale. This reduces pipe diameter and can lead to repeat clogs. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Hydro jetting is the most effective method for sewer line descaling, using powerful water jets to break down and flush away these mineral deposits, restoring the pipe's full carrying capacity and slowing future buildup.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in Layton

    Tree roots are a common problem in Layton, UT, especially where mature landscaping grows over older sewer lines. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks in pipes and grow, causing severe blockages. Hydro jetting uses a powerful stream to cut through and flush out these invasive roots. This method is far more effective than snaking for complete root removal and preventing their rapid regrowth.
