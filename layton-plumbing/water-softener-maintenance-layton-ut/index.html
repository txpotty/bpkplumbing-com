    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Layton, UT

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Layton

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle usually point to worn seals and spacers.     Repair or Replace a Softener in Layton, UT

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. Sound tanks and valves are worth keeping. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Layton

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Layton, UT

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Layton, UT

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. The bypass gets rebuilt or swapped out and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.

    Resin Rebedding in Layton

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Layton, UT

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.
