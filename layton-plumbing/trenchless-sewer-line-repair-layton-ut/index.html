    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Layton

    Before any trenchless repair, we conduct a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This key step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Layton property, avoiding guesswork.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Layton

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Layton

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Layton that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Services in Layton

    Sewer line emergencies can be messy and urgent. Our team provides emergency trenchless sewer line repair services in Layton to quickly address sudden collapses, severe blockages or major leaks.     Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Layton

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is crucial. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to precisely diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and lasting repair in Layton.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Layton, UT

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Layton

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Sectional Point Repair in Layton

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
