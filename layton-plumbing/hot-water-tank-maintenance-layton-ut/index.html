    Dependable Hot Water Tank Maintenance Services in Layton, UT

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Layton

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Layton, UT

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Layton

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Bradford White & Rheem Tank Maintenance in Layton

    We are experienced in maintaining all major hot water tank brands, including popular models from Bradford White and Rheem, commonly found in Layton homes. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements for these brands, ensuring your unit receives brand-specific care.     Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Layton

    BPK Plumbing offers a thorough tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is essential wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion.     Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Layton, UT

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Layton, UT, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.
