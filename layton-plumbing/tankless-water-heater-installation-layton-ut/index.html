    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Layton

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Layton, UT

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Layton, UT
