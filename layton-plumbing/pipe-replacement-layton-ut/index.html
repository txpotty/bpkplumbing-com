    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Layton, UT

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Layton

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    PEX Repiping in Layton

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Layton

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, notify tenants ahead of shutoffs, and test each section before moving on. Organized work.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Layton

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Mineral deposits reduce the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Layton, UT

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Layton

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Layton, UT

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Layton

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.
