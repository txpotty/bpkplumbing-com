    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Layton

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Layton

    Scheduled cleaning beats emergency calls. Roots grow back. A regular cleaning schedule keeps roots and grease in check, especially on older lines near big trees.

    Clearing Household Laterals in Layton

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots lead the list. Flushed wipes, built-up kitchen grease and root intrusion account for most backups, and we locate the problem on camera before choosing between snaking and jetting, so the fix lasts.

    Sewer Line Odor Remediation in Layton, UT

    Sewer smells mean something is wrong. Waste may be trapped. Persistent odors from drains or the yard often come from trapped waste, a blockage or a damaged line letting gas escape, and our cleaning removes the source while the camera finds any break.

    Large Commercial Laterals in Layton, UT

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Layton
