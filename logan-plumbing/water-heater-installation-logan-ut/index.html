    Expansion Tank Installation in Logan, UT

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Logan

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Logan

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Logan, UT

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Permits are included. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Logan, UT

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Logan, UT

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Logan

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Installs can happen after close.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Logan

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.
