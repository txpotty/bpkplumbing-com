    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls.     Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Logan

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else.     Jammed Disposal Repair in Logan

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller.
