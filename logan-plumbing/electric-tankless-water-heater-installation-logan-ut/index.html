    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Logan

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Mineral deposits reduce the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.
