    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Logan

    Most wet basement repairs fall between $500 and $5,000, and the spread comes from what is causing the water. Downspout and grading fixes cost the least. Interior drain systems run more because the slab has to be cut along the walls, and exterior waterproofing costs the most because the soil has to be dug out down to the footing. We give a written price first.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Exterior waterproofing and footing drain replacement mean digging down to the footing along the leaking wall. Inside repairs avoid the yard, which helps when a patio or driveway sits against the wall.

    Check the base of the walls after a heavy rain. Damp bands low on the wall and water at the cove joint are typical. With everything turned off, a moving water meter means a plumbing leak. Note where the water shows.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing covers the whole wall. It handles groundwater all around the foundation and makes sense when water comes in at many points.

    Most supply and drain repairs are finished in a few hours. Sump installs take most of a day. Stack and slab repairs run one to two days because surfaces get opened and patched.

    Tightening a loose fitting or replacing a washer hose is a reasonable DIY task. Cracked drain stacks are not. Slab leaks and sump wiring need a pro, since a mistake can flood the floor or create a shock risk.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. With wall access, plan on two to four hours. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Keep them clear and extended well away from the foundation. Test the sump each spring and swap rubber washer hoses for braided ones every five years.
