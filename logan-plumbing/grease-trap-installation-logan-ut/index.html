    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Logan

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Logan, UT

    Commissary kitchens and ghost kitchens often have several prep stations and heavy dishwashing loads. We design grease trap installs that handle high flow from multiple sinks and dish machines. Each unit is sized for the combined discharge, not just one fixture. We also install cleanouts and sample ports where the city requires them. This keeps shared kitchens compliant for each tenant.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Logan

    For high-volume commercial kitchens in Logan, large outdoor grease interceptors are often required by code. These large units are installed underground and designed to manage significant amounts of FOG before wastewater enters the municipal sewer system. We handle the entire process, including excavation, precise placement, connection, and final inspection, delivering a robust and compliant installation.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Logan, UT

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Utah ordinances for waste management.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Logan, UT

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Logan, UT. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for reliable performance and automated FOG removal.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Logan

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties. Brands like Thermaco Big Dipper are popular in kitchens with limited space and frequent cleaning needs. We install these units with the correct power supply and drain connections. They can reduce the need for frequent pump-outs in smaller kitchens. We explain daily maintenance so your staff keeps the unit running smoothly.
