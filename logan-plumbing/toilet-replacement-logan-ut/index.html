    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Logan

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Logan, UT

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.     Cracked Toilet Replacement in Logan

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it.     Toilet Brands We Replace in Logan

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. Our picks come down to flush valve size and the MaP score.     High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Logan, UT

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.
