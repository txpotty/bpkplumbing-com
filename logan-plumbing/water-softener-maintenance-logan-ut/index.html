    Control Valve and Injector Service in Logan, UT

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Logan

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Logan.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Logan

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves.     Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Logan, UT

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Logan, UT

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Logan, UT

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Logan, UT.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Logan, UT

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Logan homes.
