    Disc Faucet Repair in Logan

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Logan bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore full performance.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Logan

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from building up on your water bill.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Logan, UT

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can reduce your repair cost.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Logan

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem.     Leaky Faucet Repair in Logan

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Logan, UT

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Logan, UT

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature.     Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Logan

    The cost of faucet repair in Logan, UT typically ranges from $150 to $400, depending on the complexity of the issue and the parts required. Minor repairs like replacing a washer or O-ring are on the lower end, while more involved issues such as cartridge replacement or internal valve repairs may cost more. We provide a clear, upfront estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are best handled by a plumber.

    The most common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handles, inconsistent water temperature, reduced water pressure from the faucet, or strange noises when operating it. Any of these could indicate internal component wear or damage requiring attention.

    Low pressure at one faucet is typically a clogged aerator or cartridge. Low pressure everywhere could mean a failing pressure regulator, a supply issue or old corroded pipes.

    While a minor drip isn't usually an immediate emergency, it's a problem that should be addressed quickly to prevent water waste and potential damage. However, a rapidly gushing leak or a faucet that won't shut off completely is an emergency, requiring immediate professional attention to avoid significant water damage.

    For a standard faucet repair, where only internal components are replaced and the fixture itself remains, no plumbing permits are typically required in Logan. However, if the repair involves altering the existing plumbing lines or installing a new type of fixture that requires new connections, a permit might be necessary. We will advise you if any permits are needed for your specific situation.

    Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all major faucet brands commonly found in Utah homes, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. We keep a stock of universal and common brand-specific parts to speed up repairs.

    While some minor leaks, like a simple washer replacement on a compression faucet, can be a DIY project, most modern faucets involve specific tools and delicate parts like cartridges or ceramic discs. Incorrect repairs can lead to further damage, more significant leaks, or voided warranties. It's usually best to call a professional for a proper and lasting fix.
