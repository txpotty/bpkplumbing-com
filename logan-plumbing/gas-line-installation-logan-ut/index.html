    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Logan, UT

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Logan, UT

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, practical option for gas line installation. BPK Plumbing is experienced with installing CSST, which can simplify routing and reduce the number of fittings compared to traditional black iron pipe. We make sure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as Utah code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Logan home.

    CSST Flexible Gas Piping in Logan, UT

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing installs faster than black iron and routes easily through attics and finished walls with fewer joints. CSST must be bonded to the home's grounding system to reduce lightning damage risk. We use brands with fittings our plumbers are certified to install and follow the manufacturer's instructions.
