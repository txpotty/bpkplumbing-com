    Basement Wall Sealing in Logan, UT

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Higher elevations in and around Logan bring colder nights and longer winters, which adds extra load on water heaters and heating systems. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Logan, UT

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Logan

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Logan

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Logan

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Higher elevations in and around Logan bring colder nights and longer winters, which puts extra load on water heaters and heating systems. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Logan

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where slow leaks can go unnoticed. Backfill goes in last.

    Drainage System Integration in Logan, UT

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.
