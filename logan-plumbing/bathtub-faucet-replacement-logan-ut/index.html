    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Logan

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Logan, UT

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Logan, UT

    Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. At that stage the trim threads are often too weak and the valve body is pitted where the cartridge seals.     Replace or Keep Repairing in Logan

    Repeated repairs add up.
