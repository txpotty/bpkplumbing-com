    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Logan, UT

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a tough crust. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition.
