    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Logan

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is important for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process.
