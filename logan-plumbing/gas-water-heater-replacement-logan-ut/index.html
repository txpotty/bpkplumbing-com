    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Logan, UT House

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Logan, UT

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Logan

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Utah plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Logan

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Logan, UT

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.

    No Hot Water Calls in Logan

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Logan

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Logan, UT

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. We handle all of it. Downtime stays short.
