    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Logan, UT

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Logan, UT homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Logan

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Logan

    Gas tankless units in Logan are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Logan

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.
