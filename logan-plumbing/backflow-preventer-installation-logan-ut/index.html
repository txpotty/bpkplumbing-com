    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Logan

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Logan. Brands include Watts and Febco.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Logan, UT

    Commercial kitchens and chemical injection systems need an RPZ. Because the relief valve can discharge a lot of water, we plan the drain first, then install the assembly above grade with enough room around it for the tester to connect a gauge kit without lying on the floor in Logan, UT.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Logan

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks.
