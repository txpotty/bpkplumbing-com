    Most slab leak repairs in Logan, UT land between $1,000 and $5,000. Access drives the price. A direct repair in an open room sits at the low end, while a long reroute or a tunnel under a wall runs toward the top of that range or beyond it. You get a clear number first.

    Older copper and galvanized steel leak most, from corrosion and wear. PEX resists corrosion. Even so, PEX can leak from a bad fitting, high water pressure or physical damage, so we see every type fail and repair each one with matching parts. That's routine in Logan.

    High bills and the sound of running water with fixtures off are the usual first clues. Check for warm floors. Wet carpet, foundation cracks and a lingering musty smell also point that way, so any of them is worth an inspection. We test Logan homes for all of it.

    You can watch the water meter with all fixtures off and feel floors for warm spots. Finding the exact location takes professional detection equipment.

    A slab leak is in pipe running within or under the concrete foundation. You can't see it. Leaks in walls, ceilings or exposed pipe usually leave a stain or a drip you can follow, while a slab leak hides under the floor and takes locating gear to find.

    Hard water corrosion, abrasion where pipe rubs concrete or gravel, poor installation, high water pressure and soil movement are the usual causes. Hot water lines are especially vulnerable.
