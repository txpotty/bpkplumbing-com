    What Our Gas Water Heater Repairs Cover in Logan, UT

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Logan

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. A flame arrestor screen at the base lets air in and keeps flame from getting out. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. We replace any tripped thermal switch.

    Urgent Gas Water Heater Repair in Logan, UT

    A gas smell, a flooded garage or a relief valve blowing steam are the calls that cannot wait until Monday. BPK Plumbing runs emergency gas water heater repair across Logan, UT, with a tech who can shut the unit down, find the cause and make it safe the same day. Gas odor comes first. Walk out, leave the door open and phone the gas company from outside. After that, we handle the heater.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Logan

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. We check pressure with a manometer. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.

    Pilot Light Repair in Logan

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. With a millivolt meter, it takes about a minute to see if it is putting out enough. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Most of these jobs run about an hour.

    Burner Tray and Venting Repair in Logan

    Rust scale on the burner tray is a sign the flue baffle or the tank top is shedding. The burner comes out, the ports get cleared and the flame arrestor gets a look. Then the vent gets checked from the draft hood to the roof, because a flue that is loose, sagging or capped by a bird nest can push carbon monoxide back into your Logan home. We hold a smoke pencil at the draft hood to see which way the air moves.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Logan

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Logan, mostly in basements and utility closets. Their parts are simple, and a universal thermocouple fits nearly all of them. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. We add one during the repair.

    Hot water that quits before work on a weekday is the call we get most often. We keep the morning schedule open for no-heat calls whenever we can.
