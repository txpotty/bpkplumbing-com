    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Logan

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Logan, UT can vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150 to $350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400 to $800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosis.

    Watch for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these call for a professional inspection.

    Because of the risks that come with natural gas and possible carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Logan, UT plumbing codes.

    On average, a well-maintained gas water heater can last between 10 to 12 years. Regular maintenance, including flushing the tank, can help extend its lifespan. If your unit is nearing this age and having frequent issues, replacement might be more sensible than continuous repairs.

    Replacing a part like a thermocouple or gas valve typically does not. Replacing the water heater or altering gas piping or venting often requires a permit, which we take care of.

    Following the label instructions to relight a pilot is generally safe. If the pilot will not stay lit, or you smell gas, stop and call a licensed plumber.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.
