    Sump Pump Replacement in Logan, UT

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Logan

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Logan, UT

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Logan, UT

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Logan, UT

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Logan

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Logan

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Logan, UT

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.
