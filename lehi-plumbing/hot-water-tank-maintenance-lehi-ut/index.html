    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Lehi

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Lehi, UT

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Lehi

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Lehi home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Lehi, UT

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Lehi, UT, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Lehi, UT

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Lehi

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.
