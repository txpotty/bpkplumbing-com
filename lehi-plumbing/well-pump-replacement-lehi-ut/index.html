    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Lehi

    The cost of well pump replacement in Lehi, UT can vary widely, usually ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 or more. This price depends on factors like the type of pump, submersible or jet, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or control boxes. We provide a written, upfront estimate after diagnosing your situation.

    Most well pump replacements are finished in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. BPK Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding heavy water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement may be necessary.

    In many areas, permits may be required for major plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. BPK Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Lehi, UT so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Common signs include no water from your faucets, weak or inconsistent water pressure, strange noises from the well or pressure tank area, short cycling where the pump turns on and off frequently, or cloudy or muddy water.
