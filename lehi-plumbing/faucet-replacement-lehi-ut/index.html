    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Lehi

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. The pull-down hose wears where it bends over the spout neck, and once it splits, water runs down the inside of the faucet and into the cabinet without anyone seeing it. Sometimes a new hose is enough. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Lehi

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense lav faucets go lower, to 1.5 gallons per minute, and most people do not notice the difference because the aerator mixes air into the stream. It adds up over a year. A WaterSense lav faucet is an easy swap.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Lehi

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. The control box mounts on the cabinet wall above the floor, and it needs either a battery pack or an outlet the cord can reach, which some sink bases do not have. Power gets checked first. Sensor range is set last.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle Delta faucets use a ball valve with seats and springs, while newer ones use a cartridge, and that difference decides whether a repair is worth doing. We identify the model on site. If the body is sound, a repair may be cheaper.

    Repair or Replace a Faucet in Lehi, UT

    Repair or replace comes down to math. A mid-range faucet costs roughly $150 to $400, installation adds about $150 to $300, and two or three cartridge repairs on an old faucet can add up to the same number. We price both side by side. Discontinued parts push the decision toward a new faucet.

    Moen Faucet Replacement in Lehi

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. A stuck 1225 makes the handle stiff and the temperature wander, and after many years in place it can be cemented in by minerals. Old cartridges can crack the body coming out. That is when we replace the faucet.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Lehi

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. At that point, replace it. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Replacement in Lehi, UT

    A split hose bib often looks fine from outside. Most homeowners here winterize sprinkler systems each fall and shut off outdoor spigots to keep lines from cracking. Most splits sit inside the wall, either from trapped water or plain wear on a valve that has been cranked hard for years. We check the wall cavity. Frost-free sillcocks with built-in vacuum breakers go in as the replacement where freezing is possible.
