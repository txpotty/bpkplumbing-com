    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Lehi, UT

    Interior Drainage Systems in Lehi

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Waterproofing for Older Utah Homes in Lehi, UT

    Older basements were often built with block walls and no exterior membrane. Fast growth means much of the housing in Utah is relatively new, with PEX supply lines and plastic drains in most recent construction. Block cores fill with water and weep through the mortar joints, so we often drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores into an interior channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Lehi

    Commercial basements often hold stock, files or equipment rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Lehi

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Lehi

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where slow leaks can go unnoticed. We look at gutters and grading first, since fixing them outside often stops the seepage for far less money. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Wall Crack Sealing for Basements in Lehi

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Water Damage Assessment in Lehi, UT

    Before any repair, we figure out where the water came from and how far it spread. We map the wet zone with a moisture meter and use a thermal camera to see behind finished walls. Guessing costs money. We give you the readings and photos for your insurer.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Relief in Lehi

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.
