    Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Lehi, UT

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall. We run a 1/4 or 3/8 inch cable from the cleanout or the trap arm, and on grease-packed lines we follow up with a jetter. Chemical cleaners rarely touch grease.     Disposal Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Disposals last about eight to twelve years. Water dripping from the bottom of the housing means it is finished. We drop the old unit, check the mounting ring, install the new one and reconnect the dishwasher hose with a new clamp so it does not leak at the inlet. The old unit leaves with us.

    Kitchen Sink Installs in Lehi

    Undermount sinks hang from clips and epoxy under the stone, so we check the mounting before anything else. Old supply lines never get reused. We fill both bowls and pull the stoppers to watch for drips.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Lehi, UT

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.
