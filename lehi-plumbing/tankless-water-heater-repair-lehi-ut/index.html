    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Lehi

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. Bird nests and dryer lint at the terminal come first, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Lehi

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. Venting gets checked too. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Lehi, UT

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. We check element resistance and supply voltage. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Lehi, UT

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, yet the checks behind each one are not the same. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Lehi

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, check for a crossover valve under the far sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Lehi

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. Our tech cleans the screen, checks sensor values in the service menu and tests thermistor resistance against the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Lehi, UT

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, so the unit lights at low flow and fails when two showers run.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Lehi, UT

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. An oxidized flame rod gives a weak microamp reading and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
