    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Lehi

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. A ground microphone picks it up.     Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Lehi, UT

    Older homes in Lehi, UT often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time.     Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Lehi, UT

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. Accurate marks keep that hole small.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Lehi, UT

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Lehi

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Lehi
