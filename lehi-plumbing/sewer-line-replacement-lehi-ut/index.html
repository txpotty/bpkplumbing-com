    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Lehi

    Many older homes in Lehi may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion.     Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we plan for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Lehi, UT

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging.     Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Lehi
