    Softener Loop Installation in Lehi

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Lehi

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Lehi home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including needed bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide complete protection against hard water throughout your property.
