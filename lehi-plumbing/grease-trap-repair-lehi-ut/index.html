    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Lehi, UT

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Lehi

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Lehi

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. With old cast iron, we turn the pressure down and keep a camera on it, since a thin wall can blow out.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Lehi, UT

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Lehi, UT

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. Baffle damage often shows up at the same visit.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Lehi

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Lehi

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.
