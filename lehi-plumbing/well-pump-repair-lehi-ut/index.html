    Pressure Switch Replacement in Lehi

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    No-Water Emergency Repair in Lehi

    A dead pump leaves the house without toilets, showers or drinking water. BPK Plumbing answers emergency well pump calls throughout Lehi and nearby areas and puts them ahead of routine work. Power and the pressure switch get checked first. Those two items fix a surprising share of no-water calls without anyone having to touch the pump hanging down in the well.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Lehi

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Lehi, UT

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from about $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire size. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. You see both numbers.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Lehi

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Lehi

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. We test the motor and wire from the surface before deciding whether to pull. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Lehi

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. BPK Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Lehi, UT well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Lehi, UT

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.
