    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Lehi

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Lehi

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack.     A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Lehi, UT

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.
