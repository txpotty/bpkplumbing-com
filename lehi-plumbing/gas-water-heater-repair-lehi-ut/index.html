    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Lehi

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Lehi home. Proper venting is a critical safety consideration.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Lehi

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Lehi, UT

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is overheating, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame. These components control the gas supply and temperature. Our plumbers in Lehi, UT can test the thermostat for accuracy and inspect the gas valve for any blockages or malfunctions. We carry a range of replacement parts for various brands, allowing for fast and efficient repairs.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Lehi, UT

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is compromised due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more sensible long-term solution. We give you an honest assessment of the repair options for your Lehi, UT home.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Lehi

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Pilot Light Repair in Lehi

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Lehi, UT

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.
