    Copper to PEX Conversions in Lehi

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Lehi, UT

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Lehi, UT

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.
