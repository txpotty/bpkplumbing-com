    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter.     Tankless Water Heater Troubleshooting in Lehi

    Error codes on a tankless unit narrow the problem down, but they do not always name the part that actually failed inside the cabinet. Flow sensor faults and igniter failures can throw similar codes. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Descaling comes first, since scale causes more tankless trouble than worn parts do. Then sensors.

    Pilot Repair in Lehi

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Lehi, UT

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow tips on the flame or soot around the draft hood point to a burner starved for air. We clean the burner and check draft with a mirror or a smoke test. Backdrafting is dangerous.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Lehi

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect.
