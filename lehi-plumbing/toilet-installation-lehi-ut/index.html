    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Lehi

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Lehi, UT

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Lehi

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Lehi

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Lehi

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Powered seats with warm water and a dryer need a GFCI outlet within a few feet, and if one is not there, we line up a licensed electrician before we mount anything. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Lehi

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Figure one to two hours for a standard floor-mount toilet. Bigger jobs take a day or more. Wall-hung carriers and slab relocations open walls or floors, so they get booked as separate projects with cure time built in.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the toilet drain. Twelve inches is the most common. You will also see 10 and 14 inch rough-ins, and buying the wrong one means the toilet will not fit or sits well off the wall. Measure from the wall itself, not the baseboard.

    Two warranties apply. The toilet is covered by its maker. Parts usually get one to five years and the china often far longer, and our guarantee picks up any leak caused by the install.

    Cracks in the tank or bowl are the clearest sign. Some cracks are hard to see at first. Watch for a puddle that keeps returning or constant clogs on an early low-flow bowl with a narrow trapway. So is a toilet you have rebuilt twice in three years.
