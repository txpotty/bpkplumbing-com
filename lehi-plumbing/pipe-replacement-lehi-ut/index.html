    What Goes Into a Repipe in Lehi, UT

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Most homeowners here winterize sprinkler systems each fall and shut off outdoor spigots to keep lines from cracking. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, and when it bursts we replace the damaged section with PEX or copper and add insulation or reroute it away from the cold. Then we test it.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Lehi

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Lehi, UT

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Lehi, UT

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Some water is harder on pipe. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Lehi

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Lehi, UT

    A single bad spot can usually be fixed. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it means the rest is close behind. We show you what we find.
