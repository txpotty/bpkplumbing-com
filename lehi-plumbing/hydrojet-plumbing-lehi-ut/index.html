    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Lehi

    Residential hydrojetting in Lehi usually costs $400 to $900, including a camera inspection. Commercial lines, long runs or heavy root intrusion can cost $800 to $2,000 or more. We give you a clear price after reviewing the line.

    Repeat clogs, slow drains throughout the house, gurgling sounds and sewer odors all suggest buildup in the line. Kitchens that back up every few months are a telltale sign of grease. A camera inspection confirms whether jetting is the right fix.

    While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return. If a clog reappears shortly after our hydrojet service, we will investigate further, often with another camera inspection, to identify any structural problems that might require repair or replacement, and discuss warranty options.

    For most homes in Lehi, hydrojetting every 1 to 3 years can be helpful as preventive maintenance, especially if you have an older home, many mature trees, or frequently experience slow drains. Businesses with high-volume plumbing, like restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or semi-annual hydrojetting to prevent grease buildup.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, often leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more comprehensive and longer-lasting solution.

    It is safe for most cast iron, clay, ABS and PVC lines in decent condition. We always inspect with a camera first and lower the pressure or recommend another method if the pipe is cracked or badly corroded. That step protects weak lines.

    Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the permanent fix.

    Key signs you might need hydrojet plumbing include frequently recurring clogs that snaking doesn't fully resolve, multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, or foul odors coming from your drains.
