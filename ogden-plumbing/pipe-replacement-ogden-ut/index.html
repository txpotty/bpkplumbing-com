    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Ogden

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Ogden

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. BPK Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Ogden, UT

Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Ogden, UT

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    PEX Repiping in Ogden
