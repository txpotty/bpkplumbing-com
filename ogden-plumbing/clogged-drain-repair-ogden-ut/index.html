    Main Sewer Line Clog Repair in Ogden

    When the toilet gurgles as the washer drains, the main line is usually the problem. Every fixture empties into it.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Ogden, UT

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. The spray cleans the pipe all the way around. Cables punch a hole through a clog, while jetting removes the buildup that would start the next one. We use it on repeat offenders.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Ogden

    Drains that clog once a year can be cleaned once a year instead. That is the whole idea. BPK Plumbing sets up maintenance visits that cable or jet your problem lines, usually the kitchen line and the main, before buildup gets thick enough to stop the flow. Enzyme treatments between visits can help.
