    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Ogden, UT

    Some lines need a look first. Old pipe especially. A camera run before cleaning shows whether the line has roots, a broken section or a grease plug, which tells us whether to cable, jet or stop and talk about repair.

    Root Masses in Ogden Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. Our root cutting heads attach to the sectional cable and saw through the mass in passes, and a camera run afterward shows how much growth is left and where the roots are coming in.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Ogden

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Ogden

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Ogden, UT

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Flow slows a little each year. Cabling opens a path, while jetting strips the crust off the walls so the line stays open longer, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Ogden

    The main carries everything out of the house. A blockage there stops every drain.     Residential Sewer Cleaning in Ogden

Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.
