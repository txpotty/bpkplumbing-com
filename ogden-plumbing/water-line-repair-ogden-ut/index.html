    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Ogden, UT

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Ogden

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and longevity, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Ogden, we regularly see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our skilled plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We ensure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Ogden

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator. In Ogden, if your water pressure has dropped suddenly, it's important to have your main water line inspected.     Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Ogden, UT

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water.     Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Ogden

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    The main water line brings water from the public supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Ogden

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins.     Trenchless water line repair is a method of fixing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging.     The duration of a water line repair depends on the severity and type of repair.
