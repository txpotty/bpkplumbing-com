    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Ogden, UT

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BPK Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Ogden, UT

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Ogden, UT

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Dishwashers and boilers last longer. We schedule around your hours.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Ogden

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Ogden, UT

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.
