    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Ogden, UT

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Ogden

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Ogden

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Ogden, UT

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Ogden

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. We track that on your invoice. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Ogden

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Ogden

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Ogden, UT

    Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. By that point the threads that hold the trim are often too weak to grip a new part, and the valve body itself may be pitted where the cartridge seals against it. A new valve makes more sense.
