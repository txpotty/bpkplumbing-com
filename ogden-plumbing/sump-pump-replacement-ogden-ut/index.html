    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Ogden

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Ogden, UT usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any necessary pit changes.     Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are common signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also indicate trouble.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe, proper and effective installation.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are our pick for durability.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Ogden homes are better off with a backup system due to storm risks.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a wise investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm usually runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.
