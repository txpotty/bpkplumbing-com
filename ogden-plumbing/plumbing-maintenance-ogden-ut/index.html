    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Ogden

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Ogden, UT

    Spring snowmelt can raise groundwater and creek levels in Utah, sometimes pushing water into basements that stay dry the rest of the year. A sump pump sits idle for months and then has to work hard all at once, so we test the float switch, clean debris out of the pit and confirm the discharge line runs clear to daylight. The check valve gets a look. Battery backups get tested too.
