    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Ogden, UT

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Ogden, UT

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Some utilities offer rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Ogden, UT

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. A replacement on an existing carrier with good supply and drain lines costs far less than a new install that needs a carrier, a new 2 inch drain and a 3/4 inch supply run through finished walls. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.
