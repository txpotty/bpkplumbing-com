    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Ogden, UT

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Ogden

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Ogden, UT

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Ogden, UT

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. BPK Plumbing repairs these units in Ogden, UT. Media lasts a few years.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Ogden, UT

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Ogden

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. BPK Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.
