    Insulating Water Lines for Utah Weather in Ogden, UT

    Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. Where the line rises into a crawlspace or garage, we insulate it, and in unheated spaces we use closed-cell foam or heat cable rated for water pipe so a cold night does not split the riser at the first fitting in Ogden, UT.     PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Ogden, UT

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. For long runs, HDPE is the tougher of the two, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Ogden, UT. PEX stays covered until burial.
