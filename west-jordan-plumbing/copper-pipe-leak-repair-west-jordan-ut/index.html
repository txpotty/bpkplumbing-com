    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in West Jordan

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in West Jordan, UT

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs more involved, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. Look for white crust.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in West Jordan, UT

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. It slows the next pinhole.

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. A hot spot underfoot is common.
