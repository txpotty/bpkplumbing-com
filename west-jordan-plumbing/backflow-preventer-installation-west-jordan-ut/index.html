    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in West Jordan, UT

    Irrigation systems in West Jordan, UT are a common source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We are experienced in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants introduced by your landscape irrigation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in West Jordan

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in West Jordan, UT

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Frost-free hose bibs and well insulated pipes are common practice in Utah, especially in mountain areas with deep, long cold. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in West Jordan, UT

    For high-hazard applications in West Jordan, UT where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. BPK Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in West Jordan

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then annually.
