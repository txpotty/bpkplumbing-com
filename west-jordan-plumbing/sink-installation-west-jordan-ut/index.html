    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in West Jordan, UT

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in West Jordan

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Vessel Sink Installation in West Jordan, UT

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.
