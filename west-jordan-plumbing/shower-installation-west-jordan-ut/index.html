    Curbless Shower Installation in West Jordan

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in West Jordan

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in West Jordan

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Shower Stall Installation in West Jordan

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in West Jordan

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. A new pan, a tub conversion or a new valve and trim can change the bathroom without a gut job. You see each option's price. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in West Jordan, UT

    Budget showers can still be built right. A one-piece unit with a good valve is a fraction of the cost of tile, and set properly on mortar it holds up for a long time. Spend on the valve. You get the full price before we start.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in West Jordan

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in West Jordan

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Installation in West Jordan

    Shower plumbing for a remodel, including valve, drain and trim, usually costs $1,200 to $3,500. Full walk-in showers with tile, glass and plumbing often total $8,000 to $20,000 or more, with most of that in tile and glass rather than plumbing. Prefab units cost less.

    Kohler, Moen, Delta, American Standard and Sterling are what we install most. Any brand you prefer is fine, or we can suggest a few based on budget and features. We check lead times.

    Large rain heads and multiple outlets use more hot water. We look at the heater's size and recovery rate and tell you if it needs an upgrade.

    One to two days for a standard stall. Tiled walk-ins and showers that need rerouted plumbing often take three to five days, or more with slab work. You get the schedule before we start.

    Center drains and linear drains cover most showers. Round and square center drains work with a pan sloped from all four sides, while a linear drain lets the floor slope one way, which suits curbless showers and large-format tile. Tile size often decides.

    We do these all the time in West Jordan. The tub comes out, a new pan goes in with the drain moved to match and the valve is raised to shower height, all without a full remodel. It is much easier to step into.
