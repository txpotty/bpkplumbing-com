    What We Fix on Toilets in West Jordan, UT

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in West Jordan

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Fill Valve Replacement in West Jordan

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in West Jordan

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We wipe it all dry and watch where the first drop forms, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. If the porcelain itself is cracked, we price a new bowl rather than trying to seal it.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in West Jordan, UT

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot. With a slab floor, the flange is often anchored into concrete and can crack if the closet bolts are overtightened. We check it. If the flange is cracked, we add a stainless repair ring or replace it, then reset the toilet on a new seal.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in West Jordan

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in West Jordan, UT

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Emergency Toilet Repair in West Jordan, UT

    An overflowing bowl in a one-bathroom house is an emergency, and so is a burst supply line spraying water across the floor at full house pressure. Shut the angle stop first.
