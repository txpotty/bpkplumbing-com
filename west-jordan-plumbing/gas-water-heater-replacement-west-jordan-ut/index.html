    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in West Jordan

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in West Jordan, UT

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Some have self-cleaning dip tubes for sediment. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in West Jordan

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in West Jordan, UT

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in West Jordan

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.
