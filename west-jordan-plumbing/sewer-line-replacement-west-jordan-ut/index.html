    Partial Sewer Line Repair in West Jordan, UT

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in West Jordan

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in West Jordan

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in West Jordan, UT

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line to the street.
