    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in West Jordan

    The cost of storm drain cleaning in West Jordan usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and type of blockage, the length of the drain, and the equipment required, such as hydro jetting or snaking. More complex issues like extensive root intrusion or collapsed pipes may cost more. We provide a clear, upfront estimate before starting any work.

    Common signs of a clogged storm drain include standing water in your yard or driveway after rain, water overflowing from catch basins, unusual pooling near downspouts, or slow drainage in outdoor areas. You might also notice debris piling up around drain grates, a sign the system is struggling to clear water.

    We use a range of professional-grade equipment, including heavy-duty hydro jetting machines that use high-pressure water to scour pipes, drain snakes with various cutting heads for root removal, and waterproof camera inspection systems to precisely diagnose issues and confirm thorough cleaning.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is very effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    Common signs include water pooling around your property after rain, slow drainage from downspouts, visible debris piling up at catch basin grates, and unusual odors coming from the drains. If you notice any of these, it is a good idea to have your storm drains inspected.

    Water pooling around grates, overflowing downspouts, standing water against walls and mud stains near inlets are common signs.

    To prevent storm drain clogs, regularly clear leaves and debris from grates and catch basins, avoid planting trees directly over drain lines, and keep landscaping in good shape to prevent soil erosion. Consider installing grates with smaller openings in high-debris areas and schedule yearly professional cleanings.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may need twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.
