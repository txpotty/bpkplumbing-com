    Copper Water Line Repair in West Jordan

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in West Jordan, UT

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Fittings in the box loosen with ground movement and traffic. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in West Jordan, UT

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in West Jordan

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and if it keeps creeping we know the leak is between the meter and the house. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in West Jordan

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Water Pressure Restoration in West Jordan

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    PEX Water Line Repair in West Jordan, UT

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in West Jordan

    Price depends mostly on depth, access and pipe material. A shallow spot repair is cheap.     Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where slow leaks can go unnoticed. Tree roots wrap and crush shallow lines over time, and scale inside older pipe slowly narrows the opening until pressure drops and the wall gives out. Galvanized pipe fails most often.

    Green grass in one strip is a big one. Soggy ground in dry weather is another. You may also notice pressure dropping at every fixture, a rising water bill, cloudy water or a hiss near the meter. If you see two or more of these together, it is worth a meter test.
