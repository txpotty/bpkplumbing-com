    Water Softener Replacement in West Jordan, UT

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in West Jordan

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in West Jordan

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in West Jordan, UT

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BPK Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in West Jordan

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in West Jordan, UT

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in West Jordan

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.
