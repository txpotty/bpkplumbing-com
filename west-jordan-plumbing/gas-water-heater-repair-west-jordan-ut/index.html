    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in West Jordan

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter.     Propane Water Heater Repair in West Jordan

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. A propane tank needs its own orifice and a valve set for roughly 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. Wrong parts get swapped for the right ones.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in West Jordan

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber.     A thermocouple is a small copper-sheathed rod that sits in the pilot flame and makes a tiny electric current. The current keeps a magnet in the gas valve pulled open. No flame, no current.
