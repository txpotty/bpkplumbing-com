    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in West Jordan

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in West Jordan, UT

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in West Jordan, UT

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. Emergency calls get common parts and a descaling kit, and most heaters run again before we leave.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in West Jordan

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home.     Noritz Descaling in West Jordan, UT

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in West Jordan, UT

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in West Jordan, UT

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.
