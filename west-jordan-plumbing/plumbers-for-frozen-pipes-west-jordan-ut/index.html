    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. The crack is often inside the wall.     Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in West Jordan

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and take the coldest air directly. Brass bodies crack. We repair or replace the damaged assembly, have it tested if your water utility requires it, and show you how to drain it or fit an insulated cover before the next freeze.

    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in West Jordan

    Pipes under a slab rarely freeze, but the lines where they rise out of the slab at an exterior wall can. The riser is where trouble starts. If water bills jump or a floor feels warm or wet after a freeze, we use acoustic and thermal equipment to find a leak at or below the slab without breaking up the floor first.

    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection & Repair in West Jordan, UT

    A pipe that froze and thawed without leaking may still be damaged. A pressure test tells us. With the line isolated and a gauge on it, even a hairline split shows up as a pressure drop within minutes, and finding it now beats finding it later as a stain on the ceiling.

    Preventive Pipe Winterization in West Jordan

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze is cheaper than one burst pipe. Every exposed line gets marked. We insulate attic lines, cover vents, upgrade outdoor faucets and drain irrigation lines.
