    Why Choose BPK Plumbing for Toilet Installation in West Jordan, UT?

    Most leaking toilets we get called back to were installed by someone else with the flange too low or the bowl never shimmed. The usual shortcut is two wax rings stacked up. It fails. We size the fix to the real gap with an extender ring or a thick foam seal.

    • Right Tools, A level, composite shims and a light hand on the bolts keep the porcelain intact.
    • Level Mounting, A shimmed, steady bowl keeps its seal and does not rock when you sit.
    • Water Efficiency, A 1.28 gallon toilet uses less than half the water of an old 3.5 gallon model.

    Citywide Coverage

    Distance across West Jordan does not change how we set a toilet. Every flange gets inspected first. A condo near downtown and a house on the edge of town both get stainless closet bolts and a five-flush test before we pack up the truck.

    Experienced Team

    Each plumber at BPK Plumbing carries a level and a bag of composite shims on every toilet job. That pair heads off most callbacks. A bowl that rocks even an eighth of an inch will break the wax seal within weeks, and a shim tucked under the low side and trimmed flush stops it cold.

    Safety-First Approach

    Every install includes a new quarter-turn shutoff if the old multi-turn valve is crusty or seized. Old valves seize right when you need them. A quarter-turn valve closes in a second, so anyone in the house can stop a running or overflowing toilet without hunting for the main shutoff.

    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in West Jordan, UT

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in West Jordan, UT

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in West Jordan

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in West Jordan, UT

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in West Jordan, UT

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Smart Toilet Installation in West Jordan, UT

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in West Jordan, UT

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in West Jordan

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in West Jordan

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.
