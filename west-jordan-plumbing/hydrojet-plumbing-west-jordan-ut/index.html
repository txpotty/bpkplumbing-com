    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in West Jordan, UT

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in West Jordan, UT

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.
