    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in South Jordan

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.     Low Shower Pressure Repair in South Jordan, UT

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast.     Weak Shower Spray Repair in South Jordan, UT

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in South Jordan, UT

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in South Jordan, UT

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in South Jordan
