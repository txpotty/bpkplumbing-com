    Tank to Tankless Conversions in South Jordan

    Conversions in South Jordan usually take a full day. We drain and remove the tank, upsize and test the gas, run a new vent through a wall or roof, then mount, plumb and fire the unit with service valves. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in South Jordan, UT

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in South Jordan, UT homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.

    Residential Tankless System Upgrades in South Jordan

    Upgrading from a tank in South Jordan starts with a look at what you have now: tank size, vent type, gas line size and where the unit sits. In some homes the old flue gets capped and the new unit vents out a side wall in PVC, which is simpler and opens the old chase. Some homes need more vent pipe. The quote says which.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in South Jordan, UT

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. Their condensing and non-condensing lines both fit different homes, depending on the vent route and the budget you are working with. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing models use PVC vent and a condensate drain.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in South Jordan, UT

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in South Jordan

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in South Jordan

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in South Jordan, UT

    A tankless conversion in South Jordan, UT costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. The savings come back over time. With no standby loss and a lifespan that can reach 20 years, many homeowners see the difference on the gas bill within the first few winters. We give you real numbers.
