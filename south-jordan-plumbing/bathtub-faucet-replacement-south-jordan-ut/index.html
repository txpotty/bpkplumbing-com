    Replace or Keep Repairing in South Jordan

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. We track that on your invoice. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in South Jordan

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in South Jordan

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in South Jordan, UT

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in South Jordan

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in South Jordan

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
