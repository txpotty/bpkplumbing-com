    Clogged Toilet Solutions in South Jordan, UT

    Persistent toilet clogs are a common nuisance in South Jordan, UT homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs suggest a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers use professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in South Jordan

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores rarely stock.     Leaking Toilet Base Repair in South Jordan, UT

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal.     Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in South Jordan, UT

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in South Jordan

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Running Toilet Fixes in South Jordan

    A toilet that continually runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in South Jordan is usually caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup.     Fill Valve Replacement in South Jordan

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.
