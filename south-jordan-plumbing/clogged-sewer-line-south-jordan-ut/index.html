    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in South Jordan, UT

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. You see the footage first.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in South Jordan, UT

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close.     Grease and Sludge Buildup Removal in South Jordan, UT

    Kitchen grease cools in the sewer line and sticks to the walls. Layer after layer builds up. BPK Plumbing jets it out with a grease nozzle in South Jordan, UT. Then we show you on camera.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in South Jordan

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. It shows us everything. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Tree Root Invasion Removal in South Jordan

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture in the soil. They only need a tiny opening. Once inside, they grow into a dense mat that catches toilet paper and waste until the whole line stops flowing. We cut them out with a root saw on a sectional machine, and check the joints on camera afterward. Lining or replacement seals the joints for good.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in South Jordan

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.
