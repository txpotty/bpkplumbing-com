    Tankless Water Heater Service in South Jordan, UT

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in South Jordan

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. We follow each manufacturer's procedures so the service supports any warranty claim. Every brand differs a bit. The truck carries common parts.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in South Jordan, UT

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in South Jordan, UT

    Commercial heaters work harder than home units. Large tanks and multi-unit tankless racks see constant demand, so scale and wear build faster, and they need flushing, descaling and component checks done to the manufacturer's commercial schedule. We service them regularly. Hot water stays on for your business.

    Electric Element Service in South Jordan, UT

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in South Jordan

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. Both elements get tested. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Descaling Tankless Units in South Jordan

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.
