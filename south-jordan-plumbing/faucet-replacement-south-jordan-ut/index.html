    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in South Jordan

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Pull-down hoses crack at the spot where they flex over the neck, and the leak runs inside the body down into the cabinet where it stays hidden. Sometimes a new hose is enough. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in South Jordan

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks. At that point, replace it. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Outdoor spigots wear out from sun, hoses and hard closing. Most homeowners here blow out sprinkler systems each fall and shut off outdoor spigots to keep lines from cracking. We replace worn spigots with frost-free hose bibs where needed and add vacuum breakers for backflow protection, since code requires one on every hose connection. We also check inside the wall. A bib that leaked for months can leave wet insulation behind.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle Delta faucets use a ball valve with seats and springs, while newer ones use a cartridge, and that difference decides whether a repair is worth doing. We identify the model on site. A sound body sometimes only needs parts.

    Commercial Kitchen Faucet Replacement in South Jordan

    Commercial kitchens are hard on faucets. Pre-rinse units and hand sink faucets get swapped for T&S or Chicago Faucets models, both of which stock parts for a long time. We work around your hours. Each one is set to health and accessibility code.

    Tub and Shower Trim Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. If your valve is a Moen Posi-Temp or a Delta Monitor, a trim kit from the same brand bolts right onto it and gives you new handles, a new spout and a new showerhead. Brand matters here. An outdated valve may need replacing instead.

    Budget-Friendly Faucet Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Not every faucet swap needs a designer model. A mid-range faucet from Moen, Delta or Pfister with a ceramic cartridge and a brass body will outlast a pricey import with a plastic waterway. We will tell you where to spend. The price goes up for finish and styling, while the parts that matter stay about the same.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in South Jordan

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. The control box mounts on the cabinet wall above the floor, and it needs either a battery pack or an outlet the cord can reach, which some sink bases do not have. Power gets checked first. Sensor range is set last.
