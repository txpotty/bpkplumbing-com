    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in South Jordan, UT

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in South Jordan

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in South Jordan, UT

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in South Jordan, UT

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in South Jordan, UT

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in South Jordan

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Sump Pump Replacement in South Jordan, UT

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in South Jordan

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.
