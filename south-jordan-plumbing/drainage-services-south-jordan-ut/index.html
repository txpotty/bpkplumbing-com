    French Drain Installation in South Jordan

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after heavy rain. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or approved discharge point.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in South Jordan

    The cost of drainage services in South Jordan, UT can range from $150 to $500 for a basic clog removal, but complex issues like main sewer line clearing or hydro-jetting can cost $400 to $800 or more. Factors include the severity of the clog, accessibility, and the tools required. We provide honest, transparent pricing before any work begins.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to mechanically break through or retrieve a blockage. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, removing buildup like grease, scale, and roots, providing a more thorough cleaning. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    A standard residential drain cleaning for a single fixture typically takes about one to two hours. More complex jobs, like main sewer line clearing or hydro-jetting, can take longer, potentially three to four hours or more depending on the severity of the blockage.

    While easy to find, chemical drain cleaners are generally not recommended. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes over time, especially older ones, and can be dangerous if not handled properly. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    Watch for slow or gurgling drains, sewer odors, water pooling near the house after storms, soil erosion and cracks in walkways. Standing water in the yard for more than a day is another sign. Call us to have it checked.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from drains, water backing up into sinks or showers, and multiple fixtures draining slowly at once. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional.

    Tree roots are instinctively attracted to the moisture and nutrients found inside sewer lines. Even tiny cracks or loose joints in older pipes can allow roots to penetrate, growing larger over time and causing severe blockages and pipe damage. This is a common issue in plenty of established South Jordan, UT neighborhoods with mature trees.

    Minor drain cleaning typically does not require a permit.
