    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in South Jordan, UT

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in South Jordan

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in South Jordan

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in South Jordan

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Dry air and strong sun wear down outdoor hose bibs, irrigation parts and exposed pipe faster than in more humid places. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in South Jordan, UT

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Older homes in South Jordan may still have galvanized steel supply lines that corrode inside and cause low pressure and rusty water. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in South Jordan, UT

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in South Jordan, UT

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in South Jordan

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Drainage System Integration in South Jordan, UT

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.
