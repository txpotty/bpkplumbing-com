    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in South Jordan

    On new builds and remodels in South Jordan, we coordinate with the general contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together. That usually means a dedicated circuit for the disposal, a drain stub set low enough for the trap and a dishwasher hose path that allows a proper high loop or air gap. Early planning prevents rework.

    Standard Disposal Installation in South Jordan

    Most households in South Jordan do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in South Jordan

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in South Jordan homes.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in South Jordan, UT

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Disposal Installation in Rentals in South Jordan

    Rental owners need units that hold up to tenants. We install sturdy models with simple mounts and good warranties, and we can handle several units in one visit when you are turning over more than one apartment. Quick installs cut vacancy days. We leave a care sheet for the tenant.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in South Jordan, UT

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in South Jordan, UT

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in South Jordan

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.
