    Why Choose BPK Plumbing for Grease Trap Repair in Orem, UT?

    Missing outlet tees are the single most common repair we make. They get knocked loose during pumping more than you would think. Without the tee, the outlet draws from the top of the tank, right where the grease floats, and the trap sends grease to the sewer while looking perfectly fine from above. Replacing one is about a half-hour job.

    • Inspection Ready, We fix the items that get written up most, like missing tees and loose lids, under Utah rules.
    • Preventive Advice, We measure the trap and recommend how often to pump that fits your kitchen.
    • Specialized Tools, Jetters and sewer cameras let us find the blockage and clear it instead of guessing.

    Citywide Coverage

    Downtown restaurants and strip-mall kitchens both call us for grease trap repair across Orem. Our crews cover the whole service area. Each truck carries a jetter, a pump, spare tees and gaskets, so the plumber who shows up can usually finish the job that day. No second crew needed.

    Experienced Team

    Zurn, Watts, Schier, Thermaco and Endura traps all come through our shop. Each brand has its own weak point, and our crew knows them. Schier units are tough polyethylene but the lid gaskets wear, older steel Zurn and Watts traps rust out at the bottom seams, and Big Dipper automatic units need their skimmer wheel and heater checked. Parts get ordered by model number.

    Safety-First Approach

    Every repair meets the local plumbing code and the pretreatment rules for your sewer district. Where a permit is needed, we pull it. We also leave the trap with its sample port open and accessible and its lid secure, since inspectors check both, and a trap that works but cannot be sampled can still get written up. Small details, real fines.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Orem, UT

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. Once cracked, it leaks smell out and lets mop water into the trap. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Orem, UT

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Orem

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Orem, UT

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. A light-duty lid in a drive aisle will break eventually.

    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Orem

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We fix the failed parts and tell you plainly if the trap is too small for the kitchen.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Orem, UT

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. If we only clear the line and skip the trap, it clogs again.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Orem, UT

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. Most leaks come from the lid or the threaded inlet fitting. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. On most homes this is under an hour of work.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Orem

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.
