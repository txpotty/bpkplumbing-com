    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges.     Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Orem, UT

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic.     Slab Leak Reroutes in Orem

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Orem

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Orem homes.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Orem, UT

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Orem home.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Orem

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which particular line is compromised.
