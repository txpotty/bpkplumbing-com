    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Orem

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Orem, UT

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Orem

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Orem, UT

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Orem is an emergency for a family of five. No-hot-water calls jump the line, and the truck carries the parts that fail most: flame rods, igniters, flow sensors, thermistors and inlet filters. If a heat exchanger is leaking, we shut the water off at the service valves and order the part the same day. Control boards take longer.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Orem

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Clear the code without flushing and it comes right back, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Orem

    Navien condensing units use a secondary stainless exchanger to recover heat from the flue gas. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Orem

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Most tankless installs include isolation valves with purge ports for exactly this job, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. White vinegar will do in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.
