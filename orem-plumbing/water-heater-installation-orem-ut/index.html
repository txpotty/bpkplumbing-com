    Expansion Tank Installation in Orem, UT

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Orem

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing widely with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a safer spot.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Orem, UT

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in Utah homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these highly efficient units, ensuring proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an appealing option for many homeowners in Orem. We'll help you choose the right model for your specific needs.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Orem

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air.     Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Orem

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Water heater replacements, gas line work and new bathroom plumbing usually need a permit and an inspection from the local building department. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Orem. This small component offers significant long-term protection.
