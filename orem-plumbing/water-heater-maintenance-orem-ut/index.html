    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Orem

    While some basic tasks like flushing a tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance is highly recommended. Our technicians have the right tools, knowledge, and experience to perform thorough safety checks, spot potential hazards, and meet local codes, which a DIY approach might miss.

    Sediment buildup consists of mineral deposits, mainly calcium and magnesium, that settle at the bottom of your water heater tank. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Sediment reduces efficiency, causes strange noises, and can lead to premature tank failure if not flushed regularly.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are key for extending the lifespan of your water heater, especially where water quality varies.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In homes with harder water, flushing every 6 to 12 months can be beneficial to prevent sediment buildup.
