    Water Softener Maintenance in Orem, UT, Part by Part

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Orem

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Orem

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Orem, UT

    Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Morton and the store brands all use different valves, and parts for some of them only come through the dealer. We stock seal kits for the common ones. For a proprietary unit, we tell you up front if a part has to be ordered and how long it usually takes.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Orem, UT

    A softener stuck in backwash can run water to the drain all night, and a cracked brine tank can empty across the floor. Both are same-day problems. First, put the valve in bypass so the house still has water, then call (385) 543-4073 and we will get a plumber out.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Orem, UT

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 when they show wear.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Orem

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Orem

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Plans take the remembering off your plate. You get a reminder call before each yearly visit, and if the control valve fails between visits you get priority booking. Repairs found during a plan visit are billed at a reduced rate.
