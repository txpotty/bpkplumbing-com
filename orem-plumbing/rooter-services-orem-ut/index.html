    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Orem

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows damage.

    Tree roots are naturally attracted to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a frequent issue in Orem neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    We use industry-leading professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and durability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for prompt service with lasting results.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and generally more effective.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is clean after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, efficient service.
