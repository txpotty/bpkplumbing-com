    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Orem, UT

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Orem, UT

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Orem, UT

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Burst Main Line Response in Orem, UT

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Orem, UT

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Orem

    Strip centers, churches and small apartment buildings each come with their own wrinkles. Some have a shared meter feeding several units. Paperwork included.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Orem

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so the full line costs less than a string of repairs. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.
