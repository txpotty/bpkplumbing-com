    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Orem, UT

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Orem

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub tend to mean something is stuck in the line before the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter sized for 4-inch sewer pipe. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and the fix after clearing depends on which one we pull out. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Orem

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. Each maintenance call includes a sludge and scum reading, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Orem

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Orem, UT

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. A cracked or missing outlet tee means we dig at the outlet end alone, then cut in a new PVC tee and an effluent filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Drain Field Repair in Orem, UT

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil.
