    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Orem

    Residential ejector systems in Orem serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Orem

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Orem, UT

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. We price both options.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Orem, UT

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Orem

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs slower, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. Floating basins crack floors.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Orem

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Summer thunderstorms can bring intense flash floods, especially in canyons and on steep slopes where runoff moves fast. Battery backup is an option.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Orem, UT

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Orem

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.
