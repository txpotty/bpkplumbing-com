    Types of Toilet Replacements in Orem, UT

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Orem, UT

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Orem, UT

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. Current WaterSense models are rated at 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Some water providers offer a rebate, so ask before you buy.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Orem

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Orem

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Orem, UT

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Orem, UT

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Orem

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Orem, UT

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.
