    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Orem

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Orem, UT

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to significant damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. BPK Plumbing offers emergency repair services to quickly address these critical failures. We accurately locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to limit disruption.
