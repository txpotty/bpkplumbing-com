    What We Fix Under the Sink in Orem, UT

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Orem, UT

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Orem

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Orem

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Orem, UT

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Orem

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Orem

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Orem, UT

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Orem, UT

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
