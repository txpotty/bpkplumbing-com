    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless Installation in Orem

    A guest bath or a far kitchen sink can take a minute to get hot water from the main heater.     Electric Tankless for Guest Suites and Additions in Orem, UT

    Guest suites, detached studios and additions often sit far from the main water heater.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Orem

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. Flushing takes an hour.

    Whole-Home Electric Tankless Installation in Orem

    Whole-home electric units like the Stiebel Eltron Tempra and Rheem RTEX can feed several fixtures at once when sized right. Most need two to four dedicated 240 volt circuits, and we size from your peak flow and the temperature rise from your coldest incoming water. Dry air and strong sun weather outdoor hose bibs, irrigation parts and exposed pipe faster than in more humid places. Isolation valves go on for descaling.

    Electrical Panel Review and Upgrades in Orem

    Before any electric tankless install, we look hard at the panel. Whole-home units can need 100 to 150 amps on their own, so we check the service size, open breaker slots and existing load to see whether the house can carry it. Older panels often fall short. We bring in an electrician when needed.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Orem

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Warmer water means more flow. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.
