    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Orem, UT

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Orem, UT, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.
