    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Orem

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Orem. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Orem, UT

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Orem

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Softener Loop Installation in Orem, UT

We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service easier.
