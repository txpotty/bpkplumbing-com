    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Orem, UT

    Slab leaks can cause significant structural damage and high water bills.     Emergency Slab Leak Response in Orem, UT

    In homes built on concrete slabs, leaks under the slab can show up as warm spots on the floor, a running meter or the sound of water with everything off. We isolate the hot or cold side so you can keep some water service. Then we locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment. Repair options include direct access through the slab, rerouting the line through walls or attic, or a partial repiping.
