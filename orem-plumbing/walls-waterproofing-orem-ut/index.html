    Wall Waterproofing Work We Handle in Orem, UT

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Orem

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Orem

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Orem, UT

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Orem

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Orem

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Soils in Utah vary from clay that swells when wet to rocky benches and collapsible soils that settle when they take on water. Backfill goes in last.

    Drainage System Integration in Orem, UT

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Orem

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Orem

    Fast growth means much of the housing in Utah is relatively new, with PEX supply lines and plastic drains in most recent construction. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.
