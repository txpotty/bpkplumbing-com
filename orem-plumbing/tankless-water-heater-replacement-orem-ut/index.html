    Picking a Tankless for Your Orem, UT House

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Orem, UT

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. We work after hours when needed.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Orem

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Orem, UT

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Orem, UT

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in Orem, UT

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (385) 543-4073 any time it quits. We stock common models for same-day replacement. If your exact unit is back-ordered, a temporary repair can sometimes hold until it ships.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Orem, UT

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. We test both.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Orem, UT

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.
