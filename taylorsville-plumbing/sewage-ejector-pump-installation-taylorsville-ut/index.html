    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Taylorsville

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Taylorsville

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Taylorsville

They need a vent too.
