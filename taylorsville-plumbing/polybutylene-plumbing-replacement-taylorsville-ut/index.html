    Why Polybutylene Fails in Taylorsville

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
