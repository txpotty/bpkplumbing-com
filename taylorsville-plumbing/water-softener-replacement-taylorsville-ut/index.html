    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Taylorsville.     Dual Tank Softener Systems in Taylorsville

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Taylorsville, UT

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Softener Loop Installation in Taylorsville, UT

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that allows easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service easier.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Taylorsville, UT

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.
