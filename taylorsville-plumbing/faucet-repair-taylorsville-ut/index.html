    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Taylorsville, UT

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are experts at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Taylorsville

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Taylorsville bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Taylorsville, UT

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it hard to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer skilled repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, extending its lifespan.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Taylorsville, UT

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Taylorsville, UT

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can lower your repair cost.
