    Choosing a Backup Battery in Taylorsville

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Taylorsville

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Taylorsville, UT

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. After that, we look at why the old one quit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Taylorsville

    Spring snowmelt can raise groundwater and creek levels in Utah, sometimes pushing water into basements that stay dry the rest of the year. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Taylorsville

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Taylorsville, UT

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Taylorsville

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Taylorsville, UT

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. We route the outlet away from the house. Test them each spring.
