    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Taylorsville

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Water heater replacements, gas line work and new bathroom plumbing usually need a permit and an inspection from the local building department. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Taylorsville. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Taylorsville, UT

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often the smart move.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry serious risks if not handled correctly.     Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.
