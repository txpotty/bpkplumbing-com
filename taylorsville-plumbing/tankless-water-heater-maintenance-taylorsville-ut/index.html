    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Taylorsville, UT

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Taylorsville

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house.     Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Taylorsville

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.
