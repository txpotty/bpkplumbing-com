    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in Taylorsville

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Frost-free hose bibs and well insulated pipes are common practice in Utah, especially in mountain areas with deep, long cold. Where pipes can freeze, we install a frost-free sillcock that closes the valve inside the warm part of the wall. Every hose bib needs a vacuum breaker under code.     Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Taylorsville, UT

    Wall-mount faucets start before the drywall closes.     Pot Filler Installation in Taylorsville
