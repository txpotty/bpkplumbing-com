    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Taylorsville, UT

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Taylorsville, UT

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Taylorsville

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC.
