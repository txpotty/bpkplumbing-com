    Fixing Showers in Salt Lake City, UT

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Salt Lake City

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. We stock both. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Salt Lake City

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Salt Lake City

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.
