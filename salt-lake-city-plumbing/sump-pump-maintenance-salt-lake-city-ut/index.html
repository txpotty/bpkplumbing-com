    Sump Pump Maintenance in Salt Lake City, UT Before Storm Season

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Salt Lake City

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where slow leaks can go unnoticed.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Salt Lake City, UT

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Salt Lake City, UT

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Salt Lake City, UT

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Salt Lake City

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Salt Lake City, UT

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. Rattling or a high whine at startup usually means the motor is near the end.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Salt Lake City, UT

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Salt Lake City

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Sump Pump Maintenance Near Me With Quick Visits in Salt Lake City, UT

    Water pools in predictable places around a house: the downspout corners, the low side of the yard and wherever the grade slopes back toward the wall. We look at all of that while we are outside checking the discharge. A little regrading can cut the pump's workload.

    Local plumbers get there faster, and with a sump pump that matters, because a pit can overflow in under an hour once the pump quits during a storm. We book most pre-storm checks within a few days. Pumps that have stopped during a storm go to the front of the line.
