    Why Polybutylene Fails in Salt Lake City

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
