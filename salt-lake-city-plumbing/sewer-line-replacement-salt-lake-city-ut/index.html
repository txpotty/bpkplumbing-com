    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Salt Lake City

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Salt Lake City, UT while providing a long-lasting solution.
