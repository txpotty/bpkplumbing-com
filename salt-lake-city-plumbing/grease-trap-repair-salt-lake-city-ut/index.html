    Why Choose BPK Plumbing for Grease Trap Repair in Salt Lake City, UT?

    A trap with a broken baffle does not just stop working. It passes grease into the public sewer. That grease builds up in the main, and pretreatment programs trace it back by sampling manholes, which is how a restaurant ends up with a violation it did not know it had. Our repair is aimed at a clean sample next time.

    • Quality Parts, Manufacturer parts or commercial-grade materials that hold up to grease and hot water.
    • Inspection Ready, We fix the items that get written up most, like missing tees and loose lids, under Utah rules.
    • Accurate Diagnosis, We pump and inspect before quoting, so we quote the real problem.

    Citywide Coverage

    Indoor traps under three-compartment sinks and outdoor interceptors under the parking lot are both on our list. Both kinds come through our schedule every week. For buried tanks we bring a vacuum truck partner, and for indoor units the plumber can usually pump the trap by hand into sealed drums and carry it out the back door. Grease waste goes to a licensed disposal site.

    Experienced Team

    Diagnosing a grease trap is mostly about knowing what normal looks like. A healthy trap has a grease cap on top and clear water in the middle. Our techs measure both layers, compare them to the last pumping date, and can tell from that whether the trap is separating or just passing grease along to the sewer. Those measurements do not lie.

    Safety-First Approach

    Traffic-rated covers belong anywhere a car or truck can roll over an interceptor. A pedestrian lid will eventually break under a loaded garbage truck. We check the load rating on every outdoor lid we work on and replace undersized covers with H-20 rated castings or composite lids, because a broken lid over a deep tank is a real fall hazard. If it is unsafe, we flag it that day.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Salt Lake City

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. Hairline cracks sometimes take a plastic weld. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap. We will show you the crack before recommending anything.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Salt Lake City

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Salt Lake City

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Salt Lake City

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. The run from the sinks to the trap is where grease cools and builds up. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. A bellied pipe will keep clogging no matter how often you jet it.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Steel trap bodies also rust through at the bottom seams after enough years of hot, acidic water. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Some days the fix is a cheap gasket and ten minutes. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Salt Lake City

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.
