    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Salt Lake City

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We confirm proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out early in Salt Lake City.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Salt Lake City, UT

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Salt Lake City

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Salt Lake City

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an accurate temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or excessive energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Higher elevations in and around Salt Lake City bring colder nights and longer winters, which puts extra load on water heaters and heating systems. Properly working components are essential for both efficiency and safety.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Salt Lake City, UT

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
