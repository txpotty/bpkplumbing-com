    Tankless Descaling in Salt Lake City, UT

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. We clean the inlet filter. We also check the burner and venting.

    Electric Element Service in Salt Lake City, UT

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Salt Lake City

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Salt Lake City

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Yearly Service Plans in Salt Lake City, UT

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. We track it for you. Long-term savings add up.
