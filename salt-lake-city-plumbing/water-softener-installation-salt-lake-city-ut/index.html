    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Salt Lake City, UT

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Salt Lake City

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Salt Lake City

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Salt Lake City

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Salt Lake City, UT

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Salt Lake City

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Salt Lake City, UT

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. We remove the old unit, check the loop and drain for problems and install a newer model that fits your current hardness and household size. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Salt Lake City

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water softener installation in Salt Lake City, UT usually runs $1,500 to $4,000. System type, brand, capacity and how much plumbing work the install needs all move the price within that range. Loops add cost. You get a written quote after we test.

    With a loop in place, some homeowners can do it, but air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Hiring a plumber avoids leaks and poor results.

    Some areas require a plumbing permit for this kind of work. BPK Plumbing will tell you if you need one in Salt Lake City and can help with the application.

    Control valves usually carry about 5 years, and tanks often 10 or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale, but softeners work better on very hard water.
