    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Salt Lake City

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Salt Lake City

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
