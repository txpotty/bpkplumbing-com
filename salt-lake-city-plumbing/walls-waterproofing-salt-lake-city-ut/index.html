    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Salt Lake City, UT

    Basement Wall Sealing in Salt Lake City, UT

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Dry air and strong sun weather outdoor hose bibs, irrigation parts and exposed pipe faster than in more humid places. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Salt Lake City

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs more involved, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Salt Lake City, UT

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Salt Lake City, UT

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while repeat drywall, cabinet and floor repairs add up fast, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill.     Crawl Space Encapsulation in Salt Lake City

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Dry air and strong sun weather outdoor hose bibs, irrigation parts and exposed pipe faster than in more humid places. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Salt Lake City

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Salt Lake City, UT

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Older homes in Salt Lake City may still have galvanized steel supply lines that corrode inside and cause low pressure and rusty water. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.
