    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes.     Lateral and Spot Lining in Salt Lake City

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. A sectional liner is cheaper and quicker for a short stretch of damage, though we camera the rest of the line first to be sure it is sound.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Salt Lake City, UT

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. Open trenching under a drive or garden adds concrete, plant and sprinkler replacement to the bill, which trenchless mostly avoids. We price both side by side.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Salt Lake City

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Pipe Bursting in Salt Lake City, UT

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Urgent Sewer Failures in Salt Lake City

    Sewage backing into a tub is not a wait-and-see problem. Stop running water. Same day, we clear and scope the line, then schedule the liner or burst quickly and keep it flowing in the meantime.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Salt Lake City

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Salt Lake City yard.
