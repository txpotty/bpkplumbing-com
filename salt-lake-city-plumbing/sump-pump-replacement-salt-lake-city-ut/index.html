    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Salt Lake City for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. BPK Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Salt Lake City, UT so your entire system functions as it should. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    For added protection, many homeowners in Salt Lake City choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. BPK Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Salt Lake City, UT

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a smart habit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    Zoeller pumps are known for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Salt Lake City, UT homeowners. BPK Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a high-quality, long-lasting unit. We can replace your existing Zoeller pump with a comparable model or upgrade you to a more powerful one to meet your needs and local conditions.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Salt Lake City, UT, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.
