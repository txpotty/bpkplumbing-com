    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Salt Lake City, UT

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Basements are common in Utah homes, which places water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where slow leaks can go unnoticed. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Salt Lake City

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.
