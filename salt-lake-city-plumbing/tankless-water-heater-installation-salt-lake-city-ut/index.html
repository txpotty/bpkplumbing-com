    Why Choose BPK Plumbing for Tankless Water Heater Installation in Salt Lake City, UT?

    Tankless heaters cost more up front than tanks, so getting the install right matters. A unit with a short-cycling problem from bad sizing or a vent that ices over will spend its life throwing error codes, and the homeowner pays for that every month. We size by the numbers.

    • Lower Standby Loss, Nothing stays hot while you sleep, so standby loss mostly goes away.
    • Replaceable Parts, Parts like sensors and fans can be replaced without replacing the whole unit.
    • Long Service Life, With yearly flushing, many units last 15 to 20 years.

    Citywide Coverage

    From older homes with a gas tank in the hall closet to newer builds with the heater in the attic, we go tankless all over Salt Lake City. Closet installs often vent through the old chimney chase with a liner, while attic units need a drain pan, a service platform and a condensate line that can drain by gravity. We plan it before ordering.

    Experienced Team

    Each BPK Plumbing technician has worked through the details of tankless installs, from flame sensors to condensate neutralizers. Tankless units carry far more electronics than tanks, and a tech who reads the error history and flow sensor data can find the fault in minutes, not guess with parts. Fewer wasted parts.

    Safety-First Approach

    Condensing tankless units make acidic condensate, so we route it to an approved drain and usually add a neutralizer. Left untreated, condensate can wear on cast iron drains and concrete, and some codes require neutralizing, so nearly every condensing unit we install gets one. Refills are cheap.
