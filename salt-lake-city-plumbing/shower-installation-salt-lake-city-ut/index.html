    Walk-In Shower Installation in Salt Lake City

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Salt Lake City, UT

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. Slab homes need concrete work. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Salt Lake City, UT

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Salt Lake City

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. ADA stalls get handheld units. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Shower Stall Installation in Salt Lake City

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Salt Lake City

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Salt Lake City, UT

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. You get the full price before we start.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Salt Lake City, UT

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.
