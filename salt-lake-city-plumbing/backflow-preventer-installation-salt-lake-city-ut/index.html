    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Salt Lake City

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Salt Lake City, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Salt Lake City

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Salt Lake City, UT

    For high-hazard applications in Salt Lake City, UT where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. BPK Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Salt Lake City

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Salt Lake City. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. BPK Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Salt Lake City, UT

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Salt Lake City

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement.
