    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Salt Lake City, UT

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time.     Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Salt Lake City

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Salt Lake City

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Salt Lake City

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Salt Lake City, UT

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Salt Lake City

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. You get a repair price and a replacement price so the choice is yours.
