    Emergency Disposal Replacement in Salt Lake City

    A disposal leaking onto the cabinet floor needs to come out before the particleboard swells.     Disposal Replacement With Dishwasher Connection in Salt Lake City, UT

    Dishwashers that drain into the disposal need the knockout plug in the new unit removed first. We reconnect the drain hose with a new clamp and check for an air gap or high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from flowing back into the dishwasher during draining. Both appliances get tested.
