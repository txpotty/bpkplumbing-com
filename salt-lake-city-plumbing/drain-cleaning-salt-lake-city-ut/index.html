    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Salt Lake City

    The cost of drain cleaning in Salt Lake City typically ranges from $150 to $500, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues like main line blockages requiring hydro jetting or camera inspection might cost between $400 and $800. We always provide a clear estimate before starting any work.

    We usually advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often useless against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, flush only toilet paper, and consider periodic professional drain maintenance, particularly if you have older pipes or large trees nearby.

    Typically, you do not need a permit for routine drain cleaning or unclogging a drain. Permits are generally required for more extensive plumbing work, such as replacing a sewer line or making major changes to your plumbing system. We will tell you if any permit is needed for your situation.

    We use professional-grade equipment from well-known brands such as RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their reliability and effectiveness in tackling all types of drain blockages, for a thorough job every time.

    Hydro jetting is generally safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old Salt Lake City properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to assess pipe condition and use the safest method.

    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs usually take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.

    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.
