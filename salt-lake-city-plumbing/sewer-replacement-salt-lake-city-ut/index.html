    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Salt Lake City

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Salt Lake City homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Salt Lake City, UT

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Commercial buildings often have larger sewer lines and grease issues. We replace commercial laterals and work with businesses to reduce downtime. We may install grease interceptors as needed. We handle permits and city coordination. Our crews work quickly to restore service.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Salt Lake City

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Salt Lake City, UT

    A cleanout provides easy access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on later maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.
