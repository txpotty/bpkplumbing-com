    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Herriman, UT

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure.     Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Herriman, UT

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Herriman

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. We clean the valve body when the old part comes out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Herriman, UT

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Herriman

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.
