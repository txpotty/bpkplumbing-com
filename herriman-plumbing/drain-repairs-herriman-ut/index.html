    Recurring Clog Repair in Herriman

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and each one shows up clearly on a camera or when we pull the trap. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Herriman, UT

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs more involved, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Drain Vent Repair in Herriman, UT

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Herriman

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Remodels cause a lot of these. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, and where a vent was never installed we can add an air admittance valve in locations where local code permits it. Proper venting protects every trap in the house.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Herriman, UT

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Herriman

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Hard water makes it worse. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Figure on about an hour for a typical fix.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Herriman

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. You see puddles near the foundation. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and replace the damaged pipe so roof water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. The discharge point gets checked as well.

    Main Line Drain Repair in Herriman

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. Basement and first-floor drains go first. Roots, cracks from ground movement and collapsed sections are the common causes, and we confirm which one on camera, then choose between a spot repair, lining or new pipe based on the video. We show you before we quote.
