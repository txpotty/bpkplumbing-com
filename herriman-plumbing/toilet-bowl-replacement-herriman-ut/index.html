    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Herriman

    Freezing temperatures are a regular part of Utah winters, and pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls can freeze during cold stretches.     Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Herriman, UT

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. Replacing the bowl solves both problems.     Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time.     Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Herriman, UT

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Old cast iron flanges tend to rust through. We repair the flange with a stainless ring, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.
