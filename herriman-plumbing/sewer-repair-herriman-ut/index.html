    Sewer Camera Inspection in Herriman

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Herriman

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open.     Root Intrusion Repair in Herriman, UT

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Herriman, UT

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, with no digging at all. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Herriman, UT

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Soils in Utah vary from clay that swells when wet to rocky benches and collapsible soils that settle when they take on water. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Herriman

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Herriman

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Herriman, UT

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and show you whether a liner or a spot repair would stop the cycle for good. Mature trees in Herriman yards make this a common call.
