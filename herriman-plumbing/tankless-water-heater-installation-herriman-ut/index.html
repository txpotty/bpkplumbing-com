    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Herriman, UT

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
