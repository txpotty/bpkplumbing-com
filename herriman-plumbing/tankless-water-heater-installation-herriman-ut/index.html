    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Herriman, UT

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Mineral deposits reduce the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.
