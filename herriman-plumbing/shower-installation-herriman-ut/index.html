    Budget Shower Upgrades in Herriman, UT

    Curbless Shower Installation in Herriman

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Shower Pan Installation in Herriman

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Herriman, UT

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Herriman

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Herriman

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. Custom work can wait. We check the framing before the new unit goes in.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Herriman

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. The water heater gets sized up. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Herriman

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. The slope is our job. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.
