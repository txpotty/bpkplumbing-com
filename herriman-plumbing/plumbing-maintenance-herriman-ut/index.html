    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work.     Sump Pump Maintenance in Herriman, UT

    Spring snowmelt can raise groundwater and creek levels in Utah, sometimes pushing water into basements that stay dry the rest of the year. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Herriman.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Herriman

    Homes from the 1980s and 1990s sometimes have polybutylene piping, which is prone to failure and a common reason to repipe. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Herriman

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Utah water is among the most mineral-heavy in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.
