    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Herriman

    Mineral deposits reduce the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time.
