    Copper Pipe Replacement in Herriman, UT

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Herriman

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, and usually costs less than repairing in place. Fast too.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Herriman, UT

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, so finding one often means others are close. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Herriman, UT

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Pinholes start showing up. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Herriman, UT

    Most homeowners here winterize sprinkler systems each fall and shut off outdoor spigots to keep lines from cracking. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, so we fix it and protect it from the next freeze. Then we test it.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Herriman, UT

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Herriman, UT

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.
