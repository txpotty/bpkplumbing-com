    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Herriman

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Herriman, UT can range from $150 to $800, depending on the scope of the issue and the parts required. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might be on the lower end, while a gas control valve replacement could be higher. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Many Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves use flash codes to report problems like a weak thermopile or a tripped sensor. We read the code and test the related part.

    On average, a well-maintained gas water heater can last between 10 to 12 years. Regular maintenance, including flushing the tank, can help extend its lifespan. If your unit is nearing this age and having frequent issues, replacement might be more sensible than continuous repairs.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Most common gas water heater repairs can be finished within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, or cases where special parts need to be ordered, could extend the timeline. We strive for efficiency without compromising safety or quality.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these call for a professional inspection.

    While some minor issues might seem DIY friendly, working with gas appliances carries serious safety risks, including gas leaks and carbon monoxide poisoning. It is always best to call a licensed professional for gas water heater repair to ensure safety and proper function, and to stay within local codes.
