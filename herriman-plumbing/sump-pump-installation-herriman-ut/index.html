    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Herriman

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    BPK Plumbing installs a variety of reputable sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your specific needs and budget for your Herriman home.

    Signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, frequent cycling, visible rust or corrosion, or a pump that runs continuously without much water. If your basement has flooded despite having a sump pump, it is certainly time for a professional assessment and likely a replacement in Herriman.

    Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also warranty our installation workmanship.

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities prohibit sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, especially before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to confirm it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent unexpected failures.

    To keep your new sump pump running well, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates. Once a year, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from BPK Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Herriman home.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 6 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or if extensive plumbing and electrical work is required, the process might take a full day.
