    The cost of underground pipe repair in Herriman, UT can vary a lot, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. Factors include the pipe material, depth, length of the damaged section, the repair method chosen and the extent of property restoration needed. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends on the complexity. A minor leak repair might take a few hours, while a full trenchless sewer line replacement could take one to three days. We always aim for prompt service with minimal disruption.

    Trenchless pipe repair offers several key advantages, including minimal disruption to your yard, landscaping and concrete, which can save on restoration costs. It's usually faster than traditional excavation and less invasive, preserving the look and value of your property. This method is also durable, often extending the lifespan of your pipes.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these narrow the leak to a small area.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection promptly.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is usually responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    Look for unusually wet spots in your yard, unexplained increases in your water bill, foul odors, decreased water pressure or gurgling sounds from drains. These can all point to a hidden leak or blockage in your underground plumbing system.
