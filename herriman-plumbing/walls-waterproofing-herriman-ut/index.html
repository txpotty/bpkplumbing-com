    Basement Wall Sealing in Herriman, UT

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Dry air and strong sun wear down outdoor hose bibs, irrigation parts and exposed pipe faster than in more humid places. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Herriman

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Herriman, UT

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes from the 1980s and 1990s sometimes have polybutylene piping, which is prone to failure and a common reason to repipe. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.
