    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Herriman

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line. First we open the faucet and locate the ice. Copper and steel can be thawed with an electric machine in minutes, while PEX and CPVC get infrared or warm air, which takes longer but is safe for plastic. Patience pays here.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Herriman, UT

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a spigot with a hose still attached. A connected hose holds water in the stem. Irrigation backflow preventers sit above ground with no insulation, and their brass bodies and test cocks crack easily, so we drain or cover them before the first freeze and repair them when they split.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Herriman, UT

    Finding the frozen spot is the hard part. A thermal camera speeds it up. Fixture checks plus a thermal scan usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Herriman

    Drain lines can freeze too, mostly in crawlspaces and outside walls. A frozen drain backs up. We thaw drains with a steam or hot-water jetter fed through the cleanout, then check the slope, because a drain that holds water in a sag is the kind that freezes again next time.

    Frozen Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Herriman, UT

    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe usually costs $150 to $400. Repairing a split costs more. Burst repairs run $300 to $1,000 depending on access, and prevention work usually costs a few hundred.

    Preventive Pipe Insulation in Herriman

    Foam pipe insulation is cheap and stops most freezes in attics and crawlspaces. Fit matters. We size the sleeve to the pipe, tape every seam and miter the corners so there are no gaps at elbows and tees, since a single bare fitting is often exactly where a line freezes first.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Herriman

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. We replace old constant-wattage tape with listed self-regulating cable.
