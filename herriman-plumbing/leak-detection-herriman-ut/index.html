    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Herriman

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job.     PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Herriman, UT

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.
