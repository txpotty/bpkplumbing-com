    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Herriman

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Herriman, UT

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. BPK Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Herriman, UT

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Herriman

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. Existing loops make it faster.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Herriman

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Herriman, UT

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Softener Loop Installation in Herriman

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.
