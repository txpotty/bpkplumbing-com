    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Draper, UT

    Businesses in Draper, UT with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house.     Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Draper, UT

    Liberty builds simplex and duplex packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Draper

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Draper

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Drought years are common in Utah, so many homeowners upgrade to efficient irrigation and fixtures to cut water use. Backup power can help.
