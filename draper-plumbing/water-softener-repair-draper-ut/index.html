    Resin Testing and Replacement in Draper

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Water Softener Brands We Repair in Draper

    Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, EcoPure, Morton and GE are the softener brands we see most in Draper homes, along with store-brand models sold under other names. Our technicians know their valve designs and common failures. BPK Plumbing carries common replacement parts, so most repairs finish on the first visit without a special order.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Draper

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. BPK Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Draper, UT

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. We test it under pressure.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Draper, UT

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Draper, UT

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Draper

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Draper, UT

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.
