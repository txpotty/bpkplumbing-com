    How We Install Showers in Draper, UT

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Draper, UT

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Draper

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Draper, UT

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. Dry framing first. Then the new shower goes in.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Draper

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Draper

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Draper, UT

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. The valve is where to spend. The quote covers everything up front.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Draper

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Shower Stall Installation in Draper

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.
