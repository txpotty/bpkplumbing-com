    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Draper, UT

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Draper

    The stopper lid acts as the switch on a batch-feed unit. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Draper homes.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Draper, UT

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Commercial Disposal Installation in Draper

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually need a dedicated circuit, a control panel and a water solenoid. We set the unit on the mounting collar, run the drain to the grease trap where the local rules require it, and wire the controls so the water runs whenever the motor does. Local rules differ. The local rule gets confirmed before ordering.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Draper, UT

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Draper

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Draper

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. We install the mount, hang the disposal and connect the discharge tube to a new trap, lowering the drain at the wall if the old stub sits too high for the disposal outlet. An electrician adds power if needed.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Draper, UT

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. These units weigh more.
