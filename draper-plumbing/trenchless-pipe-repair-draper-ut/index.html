    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Draper

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Draper yard.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Draper, UT

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore. Our crew pulls new HDPE or PEX through the old path with a bursting head, or drills a fresh path with a directional boring tool, so lawns, driveways and walkways stay untouched. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Draper, UT

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Draper, UT soil.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Draper

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They hold up in tough conditions.
