    Pipe Bursting in Draper, UT

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Draper, UT

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when you count the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair. For many Draper, UT homeowners, avoiding extensive property restoration makes trenchless the more sensible long-term choice.

    Sectional Point Repair in Draper

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repair in Draper

    Sewer line emergencies, such as major backups or collapses, require immediate attention. BPK Plumbing offers emergency trenchless sewer repair services in Draper to quickly address critical issues. While trenchless methods generally take more planning, in some emergency situations they can be used to limit damage and restore function faster than traditional excavation, especially when protecting the landscape is a top concern.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Draper, UT

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Draper

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Draper homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Draper, UT

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Draper, UT

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Utah homeowners.
