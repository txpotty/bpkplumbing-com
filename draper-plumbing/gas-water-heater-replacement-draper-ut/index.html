    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Draper

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. We help you match model to demand. The install meets current code.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Draper, UT

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Draper, UT

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Draper, UT

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Draper

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. Exhaust venting gets checked against their specs.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Draper, UT

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. Then we test draft with the burner on.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Draper

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Draper, UT

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.
