    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Draper

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can stress your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Draper

    Power vent gas water heaters use an electric blower to push exhaust through PVC pipe, which lets them sit far from a chimney or roof flue. When the blower motor or pressure switch fails again and again, replacing the whole unit can be the better choice. We install new power vent models and confirm a dedicated outlet is available. Venting length and number of elbows must stay within the manufacturer's limits. Power vent tanks cost more but solve tricky placement problems.

    Tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We add our own labor warranty on the installation.

    Most cities and counties in Utah require a permit for water heater replacement because it involves gas and venting. We take care of the permit when one is required.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to cleaner combustion.
