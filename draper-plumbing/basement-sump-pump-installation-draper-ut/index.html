    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Draper

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Draper

    Protecting your family home from water damage is our priority. We install robust residential sump pump systems sized to your basement and the rainfall Draper actually gets. Our plumbers precisely dig the sump pit, install the liner, and connect the pump to your home's drainage system and power supply for reliable operation for years to come. We also make sure the discharge line directs water far away from your foundation.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Draper

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Most homeowners here winterize sprinkler systems each fall and shut off outdoor spigots to keep lines from cracking. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.
