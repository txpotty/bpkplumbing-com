    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Draper, UT

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Drought years are common in Utah, so many homeowners upgrade to efficient irrigation and fixtures to cut water use. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Draper

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Emergency Sump Pump Installation in Draper, UT

    A dead pump with water rising in the pit is a same-day call. The plumber shows up with a replacement pump already loaded, pulls the old unit, checks whether the float or the check valve caused the failure, and gets water moving again in Draper, UT. Keep a wet vac running until then.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Draper

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Draper homes. They are louder, though.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Draper

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. A pedestal pump fits where nothing else will, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. Noisy, but they last.
