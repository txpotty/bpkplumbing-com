    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Draper, UT

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Water softeners are a common fixture in Utah homes, and many newer houses are built with a softener loop already in place. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Draper

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Draper, UT

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Draper, UT homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Draper

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Draper, UT

    Upfront, a tankless install in Draper, UT costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Less standby loss, a life of up to 20 years versus roughly 10 for a tank, and replaceable parts all count over the years. Ask for both prices.
