    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Draper

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Draper, UT

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Draper

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Draper, UT typically ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required.     Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can void the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your specific warranty terms.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, strange noises coming from the unit, or error codes displayed on the control panel. If you notice any of these, it's a strong indication that your tankless water heater in Draper is due for professional maintenance.

    A typical tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, usually takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and whether any minor adjustments or repairs are needed during the visit.

    It is best to have your tankless water heater professionally serviced at least once a year. In areas with very hard water, or for heavy usage, more frequent maintenance, such as every six to nine months, might be worth it to prevent scale buildup and maintain efficiency.

    If you have isolation valves, a pump and the right solution, a handy homeowner can do it. A pro visit also checks gas pressure, venting, sensors and condensate, which a DIY flush skips.
