    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Draper, UT

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Draper

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Draper

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and reduces repair costs.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Draper, UT

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to ensure these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Draper, UT.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Draper, UT

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    As part of our maintenance, we conduct a water hardness test to verify your water softener is effectively reducing mineral content to proper levels.
