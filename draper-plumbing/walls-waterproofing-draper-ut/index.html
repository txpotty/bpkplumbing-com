    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Draper

    Many homes in Draper have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy.     Crawl Space Encapsulation in Draper

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Draper

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs slower, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Draper

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.
