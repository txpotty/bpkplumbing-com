    Shower Valve Replacement in Draper

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves accessing the valve behind the shower wall.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Draper

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Draper, UT

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be very frustrating. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower.     Shower Drain Repair in Draper

    Slow shower drains are usually clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components as needed.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Draper

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn easily again.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Draper

We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Draper
