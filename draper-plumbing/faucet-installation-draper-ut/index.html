    Utility Sink Faucet Installation in Draper, UT

    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Draper, UT

    High-End Faucet Installation in Draper, UT

    High-end faucets ship with more parts in the box.     Restaurant and Office Faucets in Draper, UT

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. We bolt the wall bracket into solid backing and fit check valves on both supplies, since a pre-rinse unit gets pulled hard all shift. Hand sinks get wrist blades or sensors. We work after close so the line keeps running.

    Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in Draper

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. Deck or wall models go in with a built-in vacuum breaker, because a hose sitting in dirty wash water can siphon back into the supply. Supplies are strapped so they cannot rub.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Draper

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. The control box mounts under the sink, up off the cabinet floor where a small leak will not soak it, and it runs on batteries or a plug-in transformer. Mineral scale can stick a solenoid open, so we add the inline strainer the maker supplies. Sensor range gets tested last.
