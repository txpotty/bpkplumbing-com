    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Draper

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the fastest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Sink Water Pressure Issues in Draper, UT

    Low water pressure at your sink can be annoying and indicate underlying plumbing problems such as mineral buildup in the aerator, faulty faucet cartridges, or even larger issues within your home's water lines. Our technicians in Draper, UT can correctly diagnose the cause of low pressure and implement the necessary repairs to restore full water flow to your sink.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Draper, UT

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once found.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Draper

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. Vessel sinks can rock on a worn mounting ring, and older porcelain basins can crack around the drain. We reattach, reseal or replace the basin as needed and recaulk with a mildew-resistant silicone. We will tell you honestly when a crack means replacement is the better value.
