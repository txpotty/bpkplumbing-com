    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Sandy

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Rocky ground can make sewer line and water service repairs more involved, and trenchless methods are often a good fit where digging is tough. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Sandy, UT

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Sandy, UT

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Scale cuts output fast. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Grease Trap Installation in Sandy
