    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Sandy

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Sandy

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Sandy

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Sandy

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Sandy

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Sandy, UT

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Sandy

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Sandy

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.
