    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Sandy

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Higher elevations in and around Sandy bring colder nights and longer winters, which puts extra load on water heaters and heating systems. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Sandy, UT
