    Everyday Sink Repairs in Sandy, UT

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Sandy

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Sandy

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Sandy, UT

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Sandy, UT

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Sandy

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Sandy

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. A down hand sink can cost you an inspection, so we work around your hours and carry commercial faucets and basket strainers. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Sandy

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Sandy, UT

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.
