    The timeline for plumbing replacement depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement typically takes 2 to 4 hours.     Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers numerous benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Sandy home.
