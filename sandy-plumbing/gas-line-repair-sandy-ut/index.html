    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Sandy, UT

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Sandy, UT

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Sandy

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Gas Line Repair Costs in Sandy, UT

    Simple connector or fitting repairs often cost $150 to $450. Interior section replacements tend to land between $400 and $1,200, and buried line replacements often run $1,000 to $4,000 or more.     CSST Repair in Sandy

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Sandy

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Sandy

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Sandy, UT

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.
