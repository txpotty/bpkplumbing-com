    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Sandy

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else.     Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Sandy

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls.     Grinding Noise Repair in Sandy
