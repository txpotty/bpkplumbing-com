    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Sandy

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Maintenance Plans for Sewer Lines in Sandy, UT

    Maintenance plans cost less than backups. Plan the visits. Periodic jetting removes grease, sludge and early root growth before it becomes a problem, which extends the life of the sewer system and avoids surprise backups.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Sandy, UT

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. A snake punches a hole. A jetter scours the full circumference of the pipe at pressures we set for the material, from PVC to clay.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Sandy

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Clearing Household Laterals in Sandy

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots come first. Flushed wipes, built-up kitchen grease and root intrusion account for most backups, and we locate the problem on camera before choosing between snaking and jetting, so the fix lasts.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Sandy, UT

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. Waste catches on them. After cutting the mass with a root saw, we jet the line to clear root hairs and leave the pipe clean, then show each entry point on camera.
