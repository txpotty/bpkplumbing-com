    The inlet and outlet baffles inside your septic tank are vital for separating solids from liquids and keeping them out of the drain field.     Drain Field Clog Remediation in Sandy

    A clogged drain field is a common cause of septic system backups and can be a serious issue for homeowners in Sandy. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction.     Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Sandy

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.
