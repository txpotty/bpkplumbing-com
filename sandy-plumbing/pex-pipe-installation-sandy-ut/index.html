    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Sandy

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Sandy

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.
