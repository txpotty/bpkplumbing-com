    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Sandy

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing.     Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Sandy

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain.
