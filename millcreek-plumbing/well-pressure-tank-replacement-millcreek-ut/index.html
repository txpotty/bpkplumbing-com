    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Millcreek, UT

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Millcreek

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Millcreek, UT

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. The diaphragm splits air from water. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, including fast cycling and water at the Schrader valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Millcreek

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Millcreek, UT

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Millcreek, UT

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. Air pressure forces water to the fixtures. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, letting water fill the whole shell and kill the air cushion. Replacement is the fix.
