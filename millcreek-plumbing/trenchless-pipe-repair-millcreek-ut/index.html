    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Millcreek

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Millcreek.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Millcreek

    We use industry-leading brands like Perma-Liner and Picote for our trenchless pipe repair projects.
