    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Millcreek

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Millcreek, UT typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    We replace all major brands of garbage disposals, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, GE, KitchenAid, and Whirlaway. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements and features of each brand, ensuring a proper and efficient replacement every time.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. BPK Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Millcreek does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We'll advise you if this is the case.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners choose 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it thoroughly. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration slightly.
