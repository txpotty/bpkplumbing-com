    Tank and Tankless Water Heaters in Millcreek, UT

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Millcreek

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Millcreek, UT

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. We carry common sizes. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Millcreek, UT

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Millcreek, UT

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Millcreek, UT

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Millcreek

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Utah adopts the International Plumbing Code with state amendments, and plumbers must be licensed by the state to do the work. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Millcreek, UT

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Your quote lists permits. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Millcreek, UT

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
