    Fixing Showers in Millcreek, UT

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Millcreek

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Millcreek

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. If the valve body is pitted or parts no longer exist, we swap the valve, often through an access opening from the closet side. New valves include pressure balancing. You can step up to a thermostatic valve for tighter control.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Millcreek, UT

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. We cut it out with an auger. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Millcreek, UT

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Millcreek, UT

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Millcreek

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Millcreek

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Millcreek

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. Replacing the cartridge solves it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.
