    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Millcreek

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Millcreek

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Underground Leak Detection in Millcreek, UT

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them difficult to detect without specialized equipment.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Millcreek, UT

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Mineral deposits shorten the life of tankless and tank water heaters alike, so regular flushing or descaling is worth the time. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
