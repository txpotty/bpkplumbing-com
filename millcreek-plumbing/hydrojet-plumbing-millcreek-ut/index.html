    Residential Hydrojetting in Millcreek

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Kitchen lines may need access under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Millcreek, UT

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Millcreek, UT

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Millcreek, UT

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Summer thunderstorms can bring sudden flash floods, especially in canyons and on steep slopes where runoff moves fast. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Root Removal Jetting in Millcreek

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Roots come back through the same cracks, so the camera shows where a repair or liner would stop them.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Millcreek

    Properties with chronic drain issues benefit from scheduled jetting. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Main Line Jetting in Millcreek, UT

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Millcreek

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.
