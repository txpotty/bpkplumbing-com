    Residential hydrojetting in Millcreek typically costs $400 to $900, including a camera inspection. Commercial lines, long runs or heavy root intrusion can cost $800 to $2,000 or more. We give you a firm price after reviewing the line.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, usually leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean.     Most homes only need jetting every few years or when clogs recur. Restaurants and busy commercial kitchens often benefit from jetting every three to six months. We recommend a schedule based on what the camera reveals.

    It is safe for most cast iron, clay, ABS and PVC lines in reasonable condition. We always inspect with a camera first and lower the pressure or recommend another method if the pipe is cracked or badly corroded. That step protects fragile lines.

    While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return.     We use professional cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID, paired with rotating, penetrating and root-cutting nozzles. Our cameras record video of the line before and after. This equipment lets us handle both homes and commercial buildings.
