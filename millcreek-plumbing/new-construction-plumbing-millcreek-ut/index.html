    Slow drains on a brand new home, gurgling fixtures, sewer odors or a stub-out that ended up under a wall are typical red flags. These usually trace back to poor slope, missing vents or layout errors before the pour. A camera inspection can confirm the cause before the problem grows.

    The plumbing rough-in phase for a typical residential build in Millcreek generally takes 1 to 2 weeks, depending on the crew size and complexity. The final fixture installation and trim-out usually takes an additional 2 to 4 days later in the construction schedule.

    We usually use PEX-A for water supply because it handles heat and resists the scale and pinhole problems copper can develop with aggressive or hard water.     Soils in Utah vary from clay that swells when wet to rocky benches and collapsible soils that settle when they take on water.     Our workmanship is warrantied, and the warranty terms are spelled out in your contract. Fixtures, water heaters and PEX systems also carry their own manufacturer warranties, often 10 years or more for PEX and fittings from brands like Uponor. We hand over the paperwork at final.

    Dry air and strong sun weather outdoor hose bibs, irrigation parts and exposed pipe faster than in more humid places.
