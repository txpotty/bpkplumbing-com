    Storm Drain Repair in Millcreek, UT

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Drought years are common in Utah, so many homeowners upgrade to efficient irrigation and fixtures to reduce water use. Debris and silt fill them up. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and replace damaged corrugated or PVC pipe so water flows to the outlet instead of pooling against the house.     Drain Vent Repair in Millcreek, UT

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Millcreek

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.
