    Why Polybutylene Fails in Millcreek

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Utah water is among the hardest in the country in many areas, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
